Gamcheon Culture Village (감천문화마을) has no entry fee and is open every day of the year. The Busan Tourism Organization lists visiting hours as 09:00–18:00 from March to October and 09:00–17:00 from November to February, and the village information center is open 09:00–18:00. The usual way in is Line 1 to Toseong Station, Exit 6, then a village bus up the hill. Everything below comes from the village’s official website (gamcheon.or.kr) and the official Busan tourism site, checked on November 8, 2026.

People live here. Saha-gu has designated the village a special management area under Korea’s Tourism Promotion Act (notice dated August 1, 2026), and the designation takes effect on January 1, 2027. Visitor rules may change after that date, so check the official notices before you go.

Why two sets of hours? They come from two sources. The official village site only gives the information center’s hours, while the Busan Tourism Organization gives seasonal visiting hours for the whole village. In winter, plan to finish before 17:00.

What the information center does

The information center (203 Gamnae 2-ro) sits near the entrance. According to the official website you can:

  • Buy the alley-tour map. It is the stamp-tour map that marks the points to walk between. The price is not listed on the website, so ask at the counter.
  • Store your bags. There is a luggage storage service.
  • Ask for a village guide. The center introduces local interpreters on request.

Haneul Maru observation deck, Gamnaegol Happiness Center and Gamnae Eoulteo run hands-on programs from 09:00 to 16:00. Resident artists’ studios ask visitors to call ahead before visiting.

Source: Official website, facilities page (Korean)

How to get there

The village sits on a steep hillside, so the standard route is subway to a Line 1 station, then a village bus (마을버스). The official site and the Busan Tourism Organization list three starting points.

Line 1 stationVillage bus to takeNote
Toseong Station (토성역), Exit 6 (in front of Pusan National University Hospital)Seo-gu 2, Seo-gu 2-2, Saha 1-1The most common route
Jagalchi Station (자갈치역), Exit 1 (Chungmu Market stop)Seo-gu 2, Seo-gu 2-2, Saha 1-1Easy to combine with Jagalchi Fish Market

  • Get off at the Gamjeong Elementary School (감정초등학교) stop and walk about 5 minutes, per the Busan Tourism Organization.
  • City bus 61 also passes the village (official website).
  • The official site spells the bus “Saha-gu 1-1” while the tourism site and bus stops say “Saha 1-1”. Same route.

Parking is tight. The official site lists two public lots.

People live here

The Busan Tourism Organization lists three points of etiquette:

  • Keep quiet and keep it clean. These are people’s homes.
  • No drones. The official website also carries a “No drones” notice.
  • Do not photograph or film residents’ private lives. Keep cameras out of doorways and windows, and do not photograph people without asking.

On top of this, Saha-gu has designated the area a special management zone under the Tourism Promotion Act, effective January 1, 2027. What the rules will be, and how they will be enforced, should be confirmed through the district office and the village website before then. This post does not describe details that have not been published.

Source: Saha-gu Office website (main banner, Korean, checked November 8, 2026)

General tips

These are not official guidance, just common-sense tips for a hillside village.

  • Wear sneakers. There are a lot of stairs and slopes.
  • Mornings are quieter. The information center opens at 09:00 and the activity venues close at 16:00.
  • Winter days are short. Visiting hours in November to February end at 17:00. If you want the sunset view, arrive by about 15:00.
  • Pair it with Jagalchi Market and Nampo-dong. The village bus goes straight up from Jagalchi Station.
  • Village buses are small and fill up fast. Expect a queue on weekend afternoons and leave some slack in your schedule.

FAQ

Is there an entry fee for Gamcheon Culture Village?

No. The Busan Tourism Organization lists it as free, with parking charged separately.

What are the opening hours?

The Busan Tourism Organization gives 09:00–18:00 for March to October and 09:00–17:00 for November to February. The information center is open 09:00–18:00 and the activity venues 09:00–16:00.

Which exit at Toseong Station?

Exit 6 of Toseong Station on Line 1, in front of Pusan National University Hospital. Take village bus Seo-gu 2, Seo-gu 2-2 or Saha 1-1.

Where can I park?

Gamcheon Culture Village Public Parking (basement of the Busan Education History Museum, 78 spaces) and Gamcheon 2-dong Public Parking (40 spaces). Both are small, so public transport is recommended.

Can I fly a drone?

No. Both the official website and the Busan Tourism Organization prohibit drone filming.

What changes in 2027?

Saha-gu’s special management designation takes effect on January 1, 2027. Check the official notices for the actual visitor rules before you go.
