The 2026 Gwangbok-ro Winter Light Tree Festival (광복로 겨울빛 트리축제) runs from Friday, December 4, 2026 to Sunday, January 31, 2027 on Gwangbok-ro (광복로) and Gwangbokjungang-ro (광복중앙로) in Jung-gu, Busan. The lights are on from 17:30 to 22:30. The festival takes over the main shopping street of Nampo-dong (남포동), so there are no tickets and nothing to book. Get off at Nampo Station (남포역) on Subway Line 1 and walk in. The details below come from the festival listing and official poster on Visit Busan, the city’s official tourism portal, checked on November 27, 2026.

The lights stay on after Christmas, right through the end of January. If you miss the year-end, you can still catch them on a January trip.

Festival dates and lighting hours

This year it starts on Friday, December 4 and ends on Sunday, January 31. That’s 59 days. The official listing gives the hours as 17:30 to 22:30.

In early December the sun sets in Busan at 17:11 (Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute, checked November 11, 2026). By 17:30 it’s already dusk, so photos come out well from the moment the lights go on. The prettiest window is 17:30 to 18:00, while there’s still a little blue left in the sky. The lights go off at 22:30, so if you want a relaxed visit after dinner, be there by 21:30 at the latest.

What you’ll see

The official listing names three things.

  • Large Christmas tree. The centerpiece of the festival. It’s drawn big in the middle of the poster too.
  • Street lighting. Lights strung overhead along Gwangbok-ro and Gwangbokjungang-ro.
  • Light sculptures. Photo spots dotted along the street.

Gwangbok-ro is Nampo-dong’s shopping street. When you get cold you can duck straight into a shop or café next door, which you can’t do at the light festivals on the beach. The whole street is the venue, so walk it from one end to the other.

December shows: Street Festa and Balcony Music Show

During the tree festival there are three Saturday shows on the car-free section of Gwangbok-ro. They’re part of the “Balcony Music Show & Street Festa” run by the Busan Tourism Organization.

DateEventTime
Dec 5 (Sat)Street Festa (parade, street performances, hands-on activities, flea market)16:00 to 22:00
Dec 12 (Sat)Balcony Music Show18:00 to 21:00
Dec 19 (Sat)Balcony Music Show18:00 to 21:00

December 5 has the “White Christmas Parade Party.” On the 12th and 19th there are live sets on building balcony stages and on a parade-car stage. The lineup is posted on the organizers’ Instagram, @starrynightbusan.

If you want the crowd as part of the fun, go on one of these three Saturdays. If you’d like quiet photos, go on a weekday evening. December 5 is the day after opening and has the parade on top, so expect it to be packed.

Getting to Gwangbok-ro

The nearest station is Nampo Station on Subway Line 1. You can also walk from Jagalchi Station (자갈치역).

FromRouteFare with transit card
Busan StationLine 1 toward Dadaepo Beach → Nampo Station₩1,600
Seomyeon (서면)Line 1 toward Dadaepo Beach → Nampo Station₩1,600
Haeundae (해운대)Line 2 → transfer to Line 1 at Seomyeon Station → Nampo Station₩1,800

Fares follow the Busan Transportation Corporation fare table (₩1,600 up to 10 km, ₩1,800 over 10 km), and a QR ticket costs ₩100 more. Once you’re out of the station, type “광복로패션거리” (Gwangbok-ro Fashion Street) into your map app and it will give you directions straight away. For how to ride the subway, see how to use the Busan subway.

Leave the car behind. Around Gwangbok-ro the queues into parking lots are long even on an ordinary weekend evening, and they get worse during the festival. For the trip back, check the last train time from Nampo Station in your map app before you set out and you’re done.

Places to combine with it

All of these are Jung-gu sights within walking distance of Gwangbok-ro.

  • Yongdusan Park (용두산공원) and Busan Tower. The hill right above Gwangbok-ro. On the night of December 31 the Citizens’ Bell ringing ceremony is held here (Busan Culture & Tourism Festival Organizing Committee, Dec 31, 2026 to Jan 1, 2027).
  • BIFF Square (BIFF광장), Gukje Market (국제시장) and Bupyeong Kkangtong Night Market (부평깡통야시장). Where to eat dinner. The street food alleys are all on this side.
  • Jagalchi Market (자갈치시장). Jagalchi Station is the very next stop after Nampo. Eat raw fish there during the day and walk over to Gwangbok-ro at sunset, and the route lines up nicely.

Busan winter light festivals compared

Lights go on all over Busan at the same time of year. If your trip is short, picking the one closest to your hotel is plenty.

Dates are from the Visit Busan festival and event calendar (checked November 27, 2026). If you’re staying near Busan Station or Nampo-dong, Gwangbok-ro is closest. If you’re staying in Haeundae, the Haeundae Light Festival is.

Tips

  • Dress warmly. Busan’s December averages are 2.0℃ in the morning and 10.4℃ in the daytime. Stand around for an hour in the evening and it’s colder than you’d think. See what to wear in Busan in December.
  • Arrive at 17:30 for the best photos. The colors of the lights show up best before the sky goes fully black.
  • Brace yourself on Christmas Eve and December 31. Those are the most crowded days. With kids, I’d go on a weekday in the second week of December.
  • January works too. The lights stay on until January 31, so you can see them on a New Year trip.

FAQ

When does the 2026 Gwangbok-ro tree festival start and end?

It runs from Friday, December 4, 2026 to Sunday, January 31, 2027.

What time do the lights come on?

They go on at 17:30 and off at 22:30.

Do I need a ticket?

No. The festival is on the street, so you just walk in.

Which subway station do I get off at?

Nampo Station on Line 1 is the closest. From Busan Station it’s ₩1,600 with a transit card.
