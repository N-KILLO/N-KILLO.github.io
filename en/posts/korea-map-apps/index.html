A map app is one of the first things to set up before a trip to Korea. In most countries Google Maps is all you need, but in Korea it’s much easier with Naver Map or KakaoMap installed as well. (As of November 5, 2026)

Why Google Maps has been limited in Korea

Korea has long restricted taking high-precision map data out of the country, and Google Maps’ route guidance in Korea has been limited as a result.

On February 27, 2026, the Korean government conditionally approved Google’s request to export high-precision (1:5000) map data, according to an announcement by the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport and same-day news reports. Google Maps may get more features in Korea, so check the app itself to see what works now.

Keep Google Maps, and add Naver Map or KakaoMap. The local apps have more detail on Korean shops, restaurants and public transport.

Search tips

General tips, not official guidance.

  • Search in Korean. English spellings of place names vary, so they don’t always show up. Every map button on this blog searches by the Korean name, so you can just tap.
  • Note the subway exit number. Korean stations have many exits. Getting the exit right makes the rest easy.
  • Save places in advance. Mobile data can slow down in big crowds, such as at festivals.

FAQ

Does Google Maps not work in Korea at all?

It does. You can view the map and look up places. Route guidance has been limited in Korea, and that may change after the February 2026 conditional approval, so check in the app.

If I can only pick one?

Naver Map if you prefer Japanese or Chinese. If English is fine, either works.

Are these the official app links?

Yes. They go to the official App Store and Google Play pages.
