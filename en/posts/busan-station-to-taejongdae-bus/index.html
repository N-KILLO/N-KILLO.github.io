Busan Station to Taejongdae by Bus: Take 17, 88 or 101 (About 40 Min), or 30 Min from Nampo-dong

2026-11-28

It’s bus 17, 88 or 101. Come out of Exit 7 at Busan Station (부산역), go to the stop across from the Kyowon Building (교원빌딩), and take whichever of the three comes first. Get off at “Taejongdae” (태종대) or at the last stop, “Taejongdae Oncheon” (태종대온천). The ride takes about 40 minutes. Taejongdae has no subway station, so wherever you start, the last leg is a bus. (Busan Infrastructure Corporation’s directions to Taejongdae Resort Park, checked November 28, 2026.)

With three numbers at Busan Station and five in Nampo-dong, you rarely wait long. Still, before you walk to the stop, tap the stop’s name in Naver Map and it shows which number is arriving first. Take that one.

When it’s better to ride the subway to Nampo Station first

If you aren’t staying near Busan Station, boarding at Nampo Station is easier. More bus numbers stop there, and the ride is about 10 minutes shorter.

  • Coming on Line 2: change to Line 1 at Seomyeon Station (서면역) and come out of Exit 6 at Nampo Station.
  • Coming on Line 3: change to Line 1 at Yeonsan Station (연산역) and come out of Exit 6 at Nampo Station.
  • At the stop across from Lotte Department Store, on the Exit 6 side, take bus 8, 17, 30, 88 or 186 and get off at “Taejongdae”.

From Haeundae it’s 1 hour 21 minutes with a bus transfer. A round trip puts you on the road for close to three hours, so Taejongdae belongs on the day you do Nampo-dong and Jagalchi (자갈치). Subway fares and how transfers work are covered separately in how to use the Busan subway.

What time should I leave in winter?

From December 3 to 8 the sun sets in Busan at 17:11 (Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute, checked November 11, 2026). The free loop bus inside the park also stops at 17:30. Add the walk up from the main gate to the 40 minutes on the bus, and I’d get on at Busan Station before 2 pm.

Check the day of the week too. The Danubi Train (다누비열차) is out of service for a safety inspection, and the free loop bus running in its place only operates from Wednesday to Sunday (Busan Tourism Organization, checked November 28, 2026). Go on a Monday or Tuesday and you walk the whole loop road. How to ride the loop bus, what parking costs and the order to see things in are all in the full Taejongdae guide.
