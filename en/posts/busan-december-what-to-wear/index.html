What to Wear in Busan in December: Do You Need a Puffer Jacket? (2°C Mornings, 10°C Afternoons, a Regular Puffer Is Enough)

2026-11-14

  • Sources

Yes, bring a puffer jacket. You usually won’t need a long one, though. December in Busan averages 5.8°C, with mornings at 2.0°C and afternoons at 10.4°C (Korea Meteorological Administration (기상청, KMA) climate normals, 1991 to 2020). A regular puffer that covers your hips, or a heavy coat, plus a scarf is enough for daytime plans. Go one step thicker only on days when you will stand by the sea for a long time, such as for night views or the sunrise.

Early, mid and late December: which outer layer

PeriodDaytime highMorning lowOuter layer
Early December (1–10)11.7°C3.4°CCoat or light down jacket + knit sweater
Mid-December (11–20)10.0°C1.7°CRegular puffer jacket or heavy coat + scarf
Late December (21–31)9.5°C1.1°CRegular puffer jacket + fleece-lined base layer, scarf and gloves

Afternoons climb to around 10°C, so you can feel warm once you start walking. That makes a knit sweater over a thin base layer more comfortable than one thick piece underneath. The subway and cafes are well heated, so pick a combination that is easy to take off and put back on.

What if I wear what I wear in Seoul?

You will be too warm. On the same climate normals, December in Seoul averages 0.2°C with mornings at -3.4°C, so Busan is 5 to 6°C higher. Walk around Nampo-dong (남포동) at midday in a long midwinter puffer from Seoul and you will end up with the zip open.

The wind is the catch. The mean wind speed in December is 3.3m/s in Busan and 2.3m/s in Seoul. The temperature looks higher on paper, but on the sand the wind hits you directly and one thin coat gets cold fast. Before you think about thickness, check whether your outer layer keeps the wind out.

Choosing by what you have planned

  • Mostly daytime sightseeing (Gamcheon Culture Village (감천문화마을), Nampo-dong, cafes): a coat or a regular puffer jacket. On days with a lot of walking, a light down jacket with a scarf is easier to carry.
  • By the sea after dark (Gwangalli (광안리) night views, Haeundae Light Festival (해운대빛축제)): a regular puffer jacket with a scarf. In early December the sun sets at 17:11, and the temperature falls quickly after that.
  • Sunrise on December 31 or January 1: this is the one day a long puffer earns its place. Sunrise on December 31 is at 07:32, and the normal temperature at that hour is around 0°C. Bring gloves and hand warmers too.

In short, put one puffer jacket in your suitcase, and bring a long one only if you plan to watch the sunrise or stay out late at night. Climate normals are 30-year averages, so any given year can be a few degrees off. Two or three days before you leave, look up the forecast for “Busan” on the KMA Weather Nuri site and check the low and the wind speed once more. If the low is below zero, dress one step thicker.

Getting to the two places in the photos

For Gwangbok-ro (광복로), the closest stop is Nampo Station (남포역) on Metro Line 1. For Gwangalli Beach (광안리해수욕장), it is Gwangan Station (광안역) Exit 3 on Line 2.
