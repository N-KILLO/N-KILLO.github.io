Planning Your Busan Trip

Can Koreans Buy the Visit Busan Pass? No, It's Foreigners Only (What Your Korean Friend Pays Instead: ₩55,000 for 3 Spots)

2026-11-21

No. The Visit Busan Pass (비짓부산패스) is for foreigners only. Travelling with a Korean friend? They can’t buy the pass and they can’t use it. People often try to buy everyone’s passes in one go and add one for the Korean in the group, but the official site says a Korean who presents the pass may be refused entry at the attraction. Buy passes only for the foreign visitors in your group, and have your Korean companion buy a separate ticket at each attraction. (Official Visit Busan Pass site, checked November 21, 2026)

The official wording

On the pass sales page, every product from the 24-hour pass to Big 5 carries the same sentence.

비짓부산패스는 외국인 전용 상품입니다. 한국인은 패스 구매 및 사용이 불가능하며 제휴시설에 패스를 제시할 시 입장을 거부 당할 수 있습니다.

(Translation: The Visit Busan Pass is a product for foreigners only. Koreans cannot buy or use the pass, and may be refused entry if they present it at a partner attraction.)

Payment is by credit or debit card issued outside Korea, and when you collect a card pass at an exchange point you have to show your passport or ID together with the voucher. Your Korean friend could spend time looking for a way to buy it in your name, but paying with your own card is the fastest way.

What your Korean companion pays

Only foreigners can use the pass, so a Korean companion buys regular-price tickets. Here are three places people often visit with the pass.

AttractionForeign visitor (pass)Korean companion (regular price)
Haeundae Beach Train (해운대 해변열차), single rideFree, Group B₩10,000
Songdo Marine Cable Car (송도해상케이블카), Air Cruise round tripFree, Group BAdult ₩19,000
Spa Land Centum City (스파랜드 센텀시티)Free, Group AAdult ₩26,000

Regular prices were checked on November 13 for the Beach Train, November 15 for the Songdo Marine Cable Car and November 20 for Spa Land (each on its official site).

Going to all three costs one Korean ₩55,000. If you do the same three on a Big 3 (₩45,000, 1 from Group A + 2 from Group B), the difference is ₩10,000, so your Korean friend doesn’t lose much by being unable to buy the pass.

What to do, by group

  • Foreign visitors only: one pass per person. Pick the type in my Visit Busan Pass prices, types and where to buy post.
  • Koreans and foreigners together: passes for the foreigners only, and regular-price tickets at the ticket office for the Koreans. Stand at the ticket office together and get it all done at once.
  • Riding the Sky Capsule (스카이캡슐) together: the Sky Capsule is not on the pass list. What the pass covers is the Beach Train. The capsule is booked separately at one fare per capsule (₩50,000 for two), whatever your nationality.
  • Not sure about nationality: for unclear cases, such as a family member who holds a foreign passport, ask the customer center (+82 1660 1122, 09:00–18:00, all year) before you buy.

Getting there: where to pick up the pass

If you arrive by KTX, collect your card at the official Visit Busan Pass counter on the 2nd floor of Busan Station (부산역) (daily 09:00–18:00). By subway it is Busan Station on Line 1. If you fly into Gimhae Airport (김해공항), you can also pick it up at the Busan Tourist Information Center (부산관광안내소) on the 1st floor of the international terminal (daily 10:00–17:00, lunch 12:00–13:00).
