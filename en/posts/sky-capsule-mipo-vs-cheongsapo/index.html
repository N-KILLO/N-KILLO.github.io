Sky Capsule: Mipo or Cheongsapo Start? Subway Access, Sunset Side & Parking Compared

2026-11-18

Contents
  • Mipo start vs Cheongsapo start, in one table
  • So where should you board?
  • Getting to the boarding points
  • Sources (checked November 18, 2026)

The fare and the ride time are the same from either end. The Sky Capsule (스카이캡슐) in Haeundae (해운대) runs the 2 km between Mipo and Cheongsapo one way only, and wherever you start it’s ₩50,000 for two per capsule and about 30 minutes one way. What changes is how you get there and which way the view faces. If you’re coming by subway and it’s your first time, go with a Mipo start (10–15 minutes on foot from Exit 7 of Jung-dong Station (중동역)). If you want to ride facing the sunset, a Cheongsapo start is the one. (Checked on the official Blueline Park website, November 18, 2026)

The track follows the coast east, and the sea is to the south. So from Mipo the sea is in the right-hand window, and from Cheongsapo it’s in the left. The sun goes down over the Haeundae Beach side, which means the capsule heading back from Cheongsapo to Mipo is the one riding toward the sunset.

So where should you board?

  • Mipo start: when it’s your first time in Busan and you’re getting around by subway. You can walk there from Haeundae Beach, and it works well to get off at Cheongsapo, look around the village, then carry on to Songjeong on the Beach Train (해변열차).
  • Cheongsapo start: when you’re riding at sunset. In winter (Nov–Feb) the last entry is 17:30–18:00, and sunset in Busan from late November through December is 17:11–17:21, so the 16:30–17:30 entry slots overlap with the sun going down. You get off at Mipo right in Haeundae, which makes going for dinner easy too.
  • If you’re driving: Cheongsapo has no dedicated parking lot. The Mipo parking lot is a tower type that SUVs and large cars can’t enter, so if your car is big, save yourself the trouble and take the subway.

The capsule boards only at the station you booked. The booking screen makes you choose between “Sky Capsule Mipo start” and “Sky Capsule Cheongsapo start” separately, so look at the departure point once more before you pay. If you’re more than 30 minutes late for your reserved time you may not be allowed to board, so with a Cheongsapo ticket it’s safer to leave plenty of time for the village bus as well.

Getting to the boarding points

Mipo Station (미포정거장) is 10–15 minutes on foot from Exit 7 of Jung-dong Station on subway Line 2. From Haeundae Station, take bus 39, 100, 141 or 200 in front of Exit 7, get off at “Mipo·Moontan Road Entrance” (미포·문탠로드입구) and walk about 10 minutes. For Cheongsapo Station (청사포정거장), take village bus Haeundae-gu 2 (해운대구2, get off at “Haeundae Blueline Park” (해운대블루라인파크)) or Haeundae-gu 10 (해운대구10, get off at “Supeo-ap” (슈퍼앞)) from Exit 5 of Jangsan Station or Exit 1 of Haeundae Station.
