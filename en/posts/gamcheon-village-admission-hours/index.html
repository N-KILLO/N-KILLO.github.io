Is Gamcheon Culture Village Free? Opening Hours: Open All Year, Until 17:00 in Winter (Nov to Feb)

2026-11-20

Contents

  • When it does cost money
  • What to know besides the hours
  • How to get there
  • Sources

There is no entrance fee, and it never closes for a day off. Gamcheon Culture Village (감천문화마을) has no ticket booth. It’s a neighborhood, and you just walk in. Visiting hours are set, though: 09:00 to 18:00 from March to October, and 09:00 to 17:00 from November to February. In winter, like now, treat 5 PM as the end of your visit and plan around that. (Busan Tourism Organization and the official Gamcheon Culture Village website, checked November 20, 2026.)

Three different sets of hours get confusing, so keep one rule in your head. In winter, arrive before 3 PM and you won’t run into either the activity cutoff (16:00) or the end of visiting hours (17:00).

When it does cost money

  • If you drive: getting in is free, but parking is charged separately. There are only two public parking lots (78 and 40 spaces), so on weekends you can spend your whole visit looking for a space. Taking a village bus from Exit 6 of Toseong Station (토성역) is far less stressful.
  • If you want to collect stamps: the alley-tour stamp map is a paid map sold at the information center. You can see the whole village on foot without it, so buy one only if you want the stamps.
  • If you stop at an activity or a studio: hands-on programs such as pottery end at 16:00. The resident artists’ studios ask you to contact them before you come.

What to know besides the hours

People actually live here, which is why the village has visiting hours in the first place. Once the sun goes down (sunset in Busan in early December is 17:11), the alleys belong to the residents and their evening, so don’t hang around late for night shots. Head back down before 17:00. Drone filming is banned, and so is photographing the inside of homes or the residents themselves.

Saha-gu has designated this area a special management area, and the designation takes effect on January 1, 2027. If you’re planning to go next year, check the notices on the official website before you leave.

Village bus numbers, parking lot addresses and what you can do at the information center are in a separate post: the full Gamcheon Culture Village guide.

How to get there

Take subway Line 1 to Toseong Station and leave by Exit 6 (in front of Pusan National University Hospital). Change to village bus Seo-gu 2 (서구2), Seo-gu 2-2 (서구2-2) or Saha 1-1 (사하1-1), get off at the Gamjeong Elementary School (감정초등학교) stop, and the information center is a 5-minute walk.
