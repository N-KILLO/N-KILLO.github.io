From Gimhae International Airport (김해국제공항), the standard way into Busan is the light rail to Sasang Station (사상역), then subway Line 2. With a transit card it takes about 26 minutes and ₩1,600 to Seomyeon (서면), about 39 minutes and ₩1,800 to Busan Station (부산역), and about 56 minutes and ₩1,800 to Haeundae (해운대). With a lot of luggage the limousine bus is easier (₩6,000 to Seomyeon, ₩9,500 to Haeundae), but the last one leaves in the 21:00 hour, so you will miss it if you land on a late flight. The numbers below are from the Busan-Gimhae Light Rail, Busan Transportation Corporation, and Korea Airports Corporation’s Gimhae Airport pages, checked on November 13, 2026.

If you are alone or travelling as a pair, light rail plus subway wins on both price and time. For three or four people going to Seomyeon, splitting an ₩18,000 taxi is cheaper than four limousine tickets (₩24,000).

Light rail + subway: change to Line 2 at Sasang

At Airport Station (공항역) in front of the terminal, board a Busan-Gimhae Light Rail train bound for Sasang (사상). Sasang Station is the last stop, three stations and 6 to 7 minutes away. Change there to subway Line 2 bound for Jangsan (장산) and the same train takes you to Seomyeon and Haeundae.

Without a transit card you pay double

This is the part that matters most. The transfer discount between the light rail and the subway applies only to transit cards. The inter-city transfer surcharge (₩500 for adults) was dropped on September 19, 2025, so with a card you pay just one fare, whichever of the two is higher. With single-ride tickets you pay the light rail fare and the subway fare separately.

TripTransit cardSingle-ride ticket (cash)
Airport → Seomyeon₩1,600₩3,400
Airport → Busan Station or Haeundae₩1,800₩3,600

The transfer only counts if you board the next ride within 30 minutes of getting off. So buy a transit card at an airport convenience store first, or tap the postpaid transit card you already use. That saves you ₩1,800.

The last light rail train runs past midnight, but the last Jangsan-bound Line 2 train leaves Sasang at 23:45. To reach Haeundae by subway you need to be on a train at Airport Station before 23:30. Later than that, save yourself the worry and take a taxi from the start.

Both the domestic and international terminals use Stop 3 on the 1st floor. Both routes are card only, no cash.

The Seomyeon bus comes about once every 80 minutes, so if you just missed one, skip the wait and take the light rail. At 26 minutes to Seomyeon, you will be there before the next limousine even shows up. The Haeundae bus drops you right at the beach entrance and in front of the hotels, so with a big suitcase it is worth the ₩9,500.

City bus 307: ₩1,550 to Haeundae

This is the cheapest way to Haeundae Beach (해운대해수욕장) without a transfer. It leaves from Stop 2 on the 1st floor.

One catch: city buses only allow carry-on size suitcases, up to 20 kg and 50×40×20 cm. The route also loops around through Gupo and Dongnae, so it makes a lot of stops. I would only suggest it if you are travelling with one backpack.

The standard taxi base fare is ₩4,800 for the first 2 km, then ₩100 for every 132 m or 33 seconds. Late at night there is a surcharge: 20% from 23:00 to 24:00, 30% from 24:00 to 02:00, and 20% from 02:00 to 04:00. If you ride to Haeundae after midnight, budget for the low ₩30,000s.

Route summary

  • Seomyeon: light rail from Airport Station (Sasang-bound) → Line 2 toward Jangsan at Sasang Station → Seomyeon Station. About 26 minutes.
  • Busan Station: go to Seomyeon Station the same way, then change to Line 1 bound for Dadaepo Beach (다대포해수욕장) and ride six stops. About 39 minutes.
  • Haeundae: board Line 2 toward Jangsan at Sasang Station and stay on to Haeundae Station. About 56 minutes. The beach is a 15-minute walk from Exit 5. The details are in my post on getting from Busan Station to Haeundae.

Which one to pick

  • Alone or a pair, one suitcase each: light rail + subway. No traffic to worry about, and the timing is reliable.
  • Heavy luggage, going to a Haeundae hotel: the Haeundae limousine. It stops in front of the hotels.
  • Three or four people going to Seomyeon: taxi. Split ₩18,000 and it is cheaper than the limousine.
  • Arriving after 21:40: the limousines are done for the day. Before 23:30 take light rail + subway, after that a taxi.
  • Early morning departure: going the other way, the first subway beats the first limousine. The first light rail train from Sasang Station is at 05:00.

FAQ

How long does it take from Gimhae Airport to Seomyeon?

25 minutes 29 seconds if you take the light rail to Sasang Station and change to subway Line 2 (Busan Transportation Corporation route search). The transit card fare is ₩1,600.

How much is the subway from Gimhae Airport to Haeundae?

₩1,800 with a transit card. You pay the ₩1,600 light rail fare first, and the ₩200 difference is charged when you exit the subway. Buying separate single-ride tickets costs ₩3,600.

Can I pay cash on the limousine bus?

No. Both the Seomyeon/Bujeon and Haeundae/Gijang routes take cards only.

Is there a limousine from Gimhae Airport to Busan Station?

There is no limousine route to Busan Station. Light rail + subway, about 39 minutes and ₩1,800, is the fastest and cheapest way.

What if I arrive late at night?

The last limousines leave at 21:30 for Seomyeon and 21:40 for Haeundae. The light rail runs until 00:07, but the last Jangsan-bound Line 2 train from Sasang Station is at 23:45, so after 23:30 take a taxi.

Can I take a big suitcase on bus 307?

City buses only allow carry-on size suitcases up to 20 kg and 50×40×20 cm. With a large checked suitcase, take the limousine or the light rail.
