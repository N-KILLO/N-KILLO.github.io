SEA LIFE Busan Aquarium (씨라이프 부산아쿠아리움) is open every day of the year, 10:00–19:00 on weekdays and 10:00–20:00 on weekends. Tickets at the door are ₩32,000 for adults and ₩27,500 for children, and online advance tickets start at ₩19,000 for children. It sits right in front of the sand at Haeundae Beach (해운대해수욕장), a 10-minute walk from Haeundae Station (해운대역). The price, hours, show and parking details below are from the official SEA LIFE Busan Aquarium website, checked on November 21, 2026.

The building has one floor above ground and three below. It is right next to the sea, but all the exhibits are underground, so it is a good place to spend time in Haeundae on a rainy day or a windy winter one.

  • Online advance tickets start at ₩19,000 for children. The official website says that is up to 31% off the door price.
  • Online tickets are sold for a set visit date. You show the QR code you receive to the staff at the ticket desk and go in.
  • You can change the date up to 5 times, until the day before your visit, and cancel until the day before too. Tickets bought on the day cannot be changed or cancelled.
  • Once you leave, you cannot go back in.

Two adults paying at the door comes to ₩64,000. If you know which day you are going, tap the visit date on the official booking page and check the adult online price for that day first. If it is lower than the door price, buy in advance, even if you do it the night before.

All of these are sold only at the ticket desk on site and cannot be combined with other discounts. Bring proof, such as an ID card, a resident register copy, a pregnancy certificate or a family relations certificate. Students who sat the CSAT (수능) for the 2027 school year pay ₩16,000 for themselves if they show their exam admission slip between November 19 and December 18, 2026.

If you qualify for a concession, compare the online price shown for your date on the booking page with the concession price and buy whichever is cheaper. Concessions are sold only at the ticket desk on site, so compare before you pay online.

Go in at last entry and you get one hour. Most of the shows finish before 16:30, so if you want to catch them, be inside by about 1 p.m. at the latest. Hours can change with conditions on site and the weather, so on the morning you go, take a look at “Today’s opening hours” (금일 운영시간) at the top of the official website.

If you only pick one, make it the 14:00 shark feeding, where three aquarists feed the sharks by hand. Watch the 13:00 mermaid show, stay on B3 for the sharks at 14:00, then catch the otters at 14:30 and the penguins at 15:30 on your way back up, and you see four shows in one afternoon.

Glass-bottom boat and VR

The glass-bottom boat (해양 탐험선) takes you around the top of the 3,000-ton main tank in a boat with a glass floor.

You wear a life jacket on board and feed the fish yourself. Pregnant women, elderly or frail visitors, people with high blood pressure or heart conditions, and anyone who has been drinking cannot ride. It is first come, first served and can sell out early, so if you want a ride, book a time slot as soon as you get in.

The VR Zone is by the exit on B1, and a session lasts 5 minutes. It runs 10:00–17:30 on weekdays and 10:00–18:30 on weekends and public holidays, and you buy the ticket at the ticket desk or at the VR Zone. Anyone 90 cm or shorter cannot ride.

How to get there

By subway, take Line 2 to Haeundae Station, leave by Exit 3 or 5 and walk 10 minutes toward the beach. For the trip from Busan Station, see my guide to the subway from Busan Station to Haeundae, with times and fares.

By bus, get off at the “해운대해수욕장” (Haeundae Beach) stop.

  • The standard rate at the aquarium parking lot is ₩2,000 per 30 minutes, and the night rate (19:00 to 09:00 the next day) is also ₩2,000 per 30 minutes.

FAQ

Can I rent a stroller or a wheelchair?

Strollers are not rented out. If you bring your own, you can get around using the elevators on each floor. Wheelchairs are available: ask at the ticket desk whether one is free and pick it up there.

Is it ever closed?

No. It is open all year. It stays open over the Seollal and Chuseok holidays too, but the hours can change, so look at “Today’s opening hours” on the official website before you go.

I can’t make the date on my ticket

Until the day before your visit you can change the date up to 5 times, to any date within 35 days of the visit date you first chose. Cancelling also has to be done by the day before. On the day itself you can neither change nor cancel.

Can I buy several tickets in one go?

Tickets bought under one booking number cannot be partly used or partly cancelled. If someone in your group might drop out, pay for the tickets separately.

What else is nearby?

Haeundae Beach is right outside the door. From November 28 the Haeundae Light Festival is on, so the order that works is the aquarium in the afternoon and the lights in the evening once you come out. Walk toward Mipo (미포) and you reach the boarding point for the Haeundae Sky Capsule.
