Can You Bring a Suitcase to Spa Land Busan? Luggage Storage Is ₩3,000 for 5 Hours

2026-11-27

  • On a 4-hour admission, storage stops at ₩3,000

  • Sources

Yes, but you don’t take it inside. You leave it in the luggage lockers (캐리어 보관함) in front of the ticket desk and then go in. The fee is ₩3,000 for the first 5 hours and ₩1,000 for each hour after that. Big suitcases are not allowed into the locker room at Spa Land (스파랜드), and the lockers inside only take personal things like a bag and your clothes. (Official Shinsegae Centum City (신세계백화점 센텀시티점) page, checked November 27, 2026)

The order is all you need to remember: suitcase in the locker, then ticket, then in. Move your change of clothes, toiletries and power bank into a small bag before you lock the suitcase. If you remember them once you are inside, you have to come back out.

On a 4-hour admission, storage stops at ₩3,000

One Spa Land admission covers up to 4 hours, and the luggage lockers start at 5 hours. That spare hour covers buying your ticket and getting changed, so you still pay only ₩3,000.

Time you spend at Spa LandStorage fee
4 hours (standard admission)₩3,000
6 hours (when you spend ₩10,000 or more inside)₩3,000 + ₩1,000 for each hour over

If you eat inside and stretch your stay to 6 hours, the locker will run past 5 hours. At ₩1,000 an hour it does not add up to much. Admission, overtime charges and parking are in my full guide to Spa Land Centum City.

How to use the half day before you leave

The gap between hotel checkout and your train or flight is when a suitcase really ties you down. At Spa Land you can drop the bag, wash and rest, so it fits the last day of a trip well. Centum City Station (센텀시티역) on Line 2 connects straight into the department store, which means you barely have to drag the suitcase outdoors.

  • Last entry is 21:00 and the doors close at 22:00. Don’t plan on leaving a bag in the lockers overnight.
  • Preschool children are not allowed in. Entry is from elementary school age.
  • If you are heading to Gimhae Airport, you can catch bus 307 at the stop in front of the department store. This bus only takes carry-on size suitcases, so with a big one go by subway and light rail instead (how to get from Gimhae Airport to downtown Busan).

How to get there

Get off at Centum City Station on subway Line 2 and the station leads straight into the Shinsegae department store. It is three stops from Haeundae Station. Spa Land is on the 1st floor of the department store.
