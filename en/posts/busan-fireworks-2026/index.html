The 21st Busan Fireworks Festival takes place on Saturday, November 7, 2026, across Gwangalli Beach, Igidae and Dongbaekseom Island. The main show, the Busan Multi Fireworks Show, runs 19:25–19:55. Everything below is based on the official website (busanfireworks.com), checked on November 5, 2026.

The organizer notes that the schedule may change. Check the official site once more before you head out.

Road closures

Busan City announced that Gwangan Bridge (Gwangandaegyo) is under traffic control from November 2 to November 8. For road closures around the venue on the day, see the Busan City’s traffic control notice for the 21st festival (in Korean).

Tickets

You can watch for free from the beach, or buy a paid ticket for a reserved seat.

  • Regular tickets (NOL / NOL World): sales close Friday, November 6 at 17:00 KST, the day before the festival.
  • Early-bird tickets and accommodation packages are already sold out or closed.
  • The organizer warns against buying resold tickets from unofficial sellers.

Source: official ticket page

Local tips

These are personal tips, not official guidance.

  • Go early. Good spots on the beach fill up from the afternoon. The daytime program (Fireworks Street, the 16:00 air show) is a nice way to wait.
  • November nights by the sea are cold. Bring a blanket, hand warmers and a mat to sit on.
  • Charge your phone. Mobile data gets slow in big crowds. Agree on a meeting point with your group in advance.
  • Getting home is the hardest part. Everyone heads to the subway right after 20:00. Having dinner nearby and leaving later is often easier.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. You can watch from anywhere along Gwangalli Beach, Igidae and Dongbaekseom. Paid tickets are only for reserved seating.

What time is the main show?

The Busan Multi Fireworks Show runs from 19:25 to 19:55.

Can I drive?

Not recommended. There is no parking near the venues and Gwangan Bridge is under traffic control.
