**A round trip on the Songdo Marine Cable Car (송도해상케이블카) costs ₩19,000 per adult in a regular cabin, called Air Cruise (에어크루즈), and ₩24,000 in a glass-floor cabin, called Crystal Cruise (크리스탈크루즈).** It runs 09:00–21:00 in November and closes at 20:00 from December through February, and the ticket office stops selling 30 minutes before closing. You can buy tickets on the spot without booking. The numbers below are from the official Songdo Marine Cable Car site, checked on November 15, 2026.

## At a glance

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Route | Songdo Bay Station (송도베이스테이션, Songnim Park) ↔ Songdo Sky Park (송도스카이파크, Amnam Park), 1.62 km over the sea |
| Round trip | Air Cruise adult ₩19,000 · Crystal Cruise adult ₩24,000 |
| Hours | Mar–Jun, Sep–Nov 09:00–21:00 / Dec–Feb 09:00–20:00 / Jul–Aug 09:00–22:00 |
| Last ticket sales | 30 minutes before closing |
| Ride time | About 15–20 minutes round trip, 60–90 minutes with the facilities |
| Scheduled maintenance | Open 12:00–21:00 on Monday, November 30, 2026 and 12:00–20:00 on Tuesday, December 1 |

The cable car crosses the sea at up to 86 m, from Songnim Park (송림공원) at the east end of Songdo Beach to Amnam Park (암남공원) at the west end. One cabin holds 8 people, and when it is busy you share with other groups.

## Prices: Air Cruise vs Crystal Cruise

| Ticket | Type | Adult | Child |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Air Cruise** (regular cabin) | Round trip | ₩19,000 | ₩14,000 |
| | One way | ₩15,000 | ₩12,000 |
| **Crystal Cruise** (glass floor) | Round trip | ₩24,000 | ₩18,000 |
| | One way | ₩19,000 | ₩15,000 |
| **Unlimited day pass** (weekdays only) | | ₩35,000 | ₩30,000 |

- Adult means middle school age (13) and up. Child means 36 months through elementary school age (12). **Under 36 months is free.**
- **Speedy Cruise**, which lets you board without queuing, is ₩55,000 per person for Crystal and ₩45,000 for Air, the same for adults and children.
- **Premium Cruise** gives you a whole cabin to yourselves: ₩340,000 for Crystal, ₩270,000 for Air, up to 8 people.

One way is only ₩4,000–5,000 cheaper than a round trip. A one-way ticket can **only be used at the station where you bought it**, so unless you plan to walk back from the other side, get the round trip. If you want to look down at the sea through the floor, pay the extra ₩5,000 for Crystal. If someone in your group is afraid of heights, go with Air Cruise.

### Who gets a discount

| Who | Discount |
|---|---|
| Seniors (65+), national merit recipients, people with disabilities, multi-child families, Munhwa Nuri Card holders | ₩2,000 |
| Busan residents (weekdays only) | Crystal ₩4,000 · Air ₩3,000 |
| Residents of Seo-gu, Busan | Crystal ₩6,000 · Air ₩5,000 |

The discounts apply only to Air Cruise and Crystal Cruise tickets, and you have to show ID or other proof at the ticket office. They cannot be combined with other promotions. If you live in Busan, bring your ID on a weekday and a round trip is ₩16,000.

If there are non-Korean visitors in your group, the Songdo Marine Cable Car is one of the Group B free attractions on the [Visit Busan Pass]({{< relref "/posts/visit-busan-pass-guide" >}}), so check whether the pass works out cheaper.

## Hours, and the November 30 and December 1 maintenance

| Period | Hours |
|---|---|
| March–June, September–November | 09:00–21:00 |
| December, January–February | 09:00–20:00 |
| July–August | 09:00–22:00 |

Tickets are sold until 30 minutes before closing. That makes the last sale 20:30 in November and 19:30 in December.

Because of the **fourth-quarter scheduled maintenance**, the cable car runs only 12:00–21:00 on Monday, November 30, 2026 and 12:00–20:00 on Tuesday, December 1. You cannot ride on those two mornings. On days with strong wind or bad weather, service can stop or end early, and unused tickets are refunded when that happens.

The front page of the official site shows that day's hours and the sunset time. On November 15 sunset was at 17:18. To get both the sunset and the night view, cross at around 16:30, watch the sun go down from the observatory on the Amnam Park side, and come back after the lights are on. In December it closes at 20:00, so if you go too late you will be cutting it close for a cabin back.

## Booking online vs buying at the ticket office

- **You can always buy at the ticket office.** You can ride without a reservation.
- **Tickets booked online can be used from the day after you buy them.** You cannot buy one and use it the same day.
- For cancellations and refunds of online bookings, contact the seller (the official booking partner is NOL Universe, 1599-8370).
- A ticket can be used and refunded **only on the day it is for**.

If you decided to go today, skip the booking screen and head for the ticket office. On weekends the front page of the official site shows the boarding queue number, so look at it before you leave.

## Where you board, and what's on the other side

| Station | Address | Main facilities |
|---|---|---|
| **Songdo Bay Station** | 171 Songdohaebyeon-ro, Seo-gu (서구 송도해변로 171) | Ticket office on the 1st floor, boarding platform on the 4th, Bay Terrace observatory on the 3rd, cafe and convenience store |
| **Songdo Sky Park** | 181 Amnamgongwon-ro, Seo-gu (서구 암남공원로 181) | Ticket office and boarding platform on the 1st floor, Sky Harbor observatory on the 3rd, Cable Car Museum on basement level 1, snack shops |

Most people start from **Songdo Bay Station** on the Songdo Beach side, because the bus stops and the parking lot with free parking for cable car riders are on this side. Over at Sky Park you get the Sky Harbor observatory, Dino Adventure with its dinosaur models, and a Little Prince photo zone. With a round-trip ticket you can stay as long as you like within that day's opening hours. They check your ticket again on the way back, so **don't throw your boarding pass away.**

Songdo Beach is right outside Bay Station, and Songdo Cloud Trails (송도구름산책로), a walkway out over the sea, is at the east end of the beach. It is an easy walk to add before or after the ride.

## Things to know before you board

- **No food or drink in the cabins.** Eat your snacks at the shops in either station before you get on.
- **Pets** ride for free. They have to be in a carrier or pet stroller with their head inside.
- **Strollers** go on folded. A **regular wheelchair** can roll straight on with you in it. Electric wheelchairs are restricted depending on size and shape, so call the main line (051-247-9900) ahead of time.
- Round-trip riders also **have to get off** on the other side and board again. You cannot stay in the cabin for a loop.

These are the bus routes the official site gives.

| From | Bus | Get off at | Walk |

| Busan Station | 17, 61 | Songdo Entrance stop (송도입구) | 15 min |
| Busan Station | 26 | Amnam-dong Community Center stop (암남동주민센터) | 10 min |

Songdo has no subway station. The usual way is to take subway Line 1 to Nampo Station or Jagalchi Station and change to a bus, and if you want less walking, pick a bus that stops at **Amnam-dong Community Center**. It fits well as the afternoon half of a day spent around Nampo-dong and Jagalchi Market.

|---|---|---|---|
| **Namhang Parking Lot** (남항주차장, 40 Chungmu-daero 94beon-gil, Seo-gu) | ₩300 | ₩500 | 1 hour free on weekdays, 2 hours free on weekends and public holidays |
| **Songnim Parking Lot** (송림주차장, 171 Songdohaebyeon-ro, Seo-gu) | ₩500 | ₩500 | Ask at the ticket office |
| **Amnam Park Public Parking Lot** (암남공원 공영주차장, Sky Park side) | ₩300 | ₩300 | No free parking |

With the unlimited day pass, free time at Namhang Parking Lot goes up to 2 hours on weekdays and 4 hours on weekends and public holidays. The official guide also recommends Namhang Parking Lot on weekends and public holidays. If you only ride there and back you will finish inside the free time, but a long stay on the other side will run over. If you are driving on a weekend, park at Namhang and plan around 2 hours.

## FAQ

### Do I need a reservation for the Songdo Marine Cable Car?

No. The ticket office sells tickets all day, and a ticket booked online cannot be used until the day after you buy it.

### How long does the ride take?

About 15–20 minutes for a round trip. Allow 60–90 minutes if you also see the observatories and themed areas.

### What is different about Crystal Cruise?

The cabin has a glass floor. A round trip costs ₩5,000 more than Air Cruise for adults and ₩4,000 more for children.

### How late does it run in December?

From December through February it is 09:00–20:00, and ticket sales end at 19:30. I covered December weather and what to wear in [Busan weather in November and December]({{< relref "/posts/busan-november-december-weather" >}}).

### Does it run in rain or wind?

In bad weather it can stop or change its hours. On the morning you go, check the front page of the official site or call the main line at 051-247-9900.
