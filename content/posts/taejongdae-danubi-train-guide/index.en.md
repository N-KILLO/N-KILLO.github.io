**Taejongdae (태종대) is free to enter. The Danubi Train (다누비열차) is not running right now because of a safety inspection, and a free loop bus runs in its place from Wednesday to Sunday, 09:30–17:30. On Mondays and Tuesdays the bus is off too.** From Busan Station (부산역) it is about 40 minutes on bus 17, 88 or 101, and about 30 minutes from Nampo-dong (남포동). Everything below is from the websites of the Busan Tourism Organization, which runs the Danubi Train, and the Busan Infrastructure Corporation, which manages the park, checked on November 28, 2026.

## Is the Taejongdae Danubi Train running now?

No. The Busan Tourism Organization's notice says "The Danubi Train is currently not operating due to a safety inspection," and for when it comes back it only says "until the safety inspection is finished." Until then the free loop bus covers the same road.

## How to ride the free Taejongdae loop bus

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Fare | Free |
| Operating days | Wednesday to Sunday |
| Days off | Every Monday and Tuesday (can change over long holidays) |
| Ticket pick-up hours | 09:10–17:30 |
| Operating hours | 09:30–17:30 |
| Vehicles | One 30-seater, two 46-seaters |
| Where to board | Danubi Plaza ticket office (다누비광장 매표소) |

You pick up a free ticket for a specific departure at the ticket office and board with it. It is first come, first served, so once the seats are gone that departure is closed. The ticket office and the boarding platform are about 200 m uphill on foot from the tourist information office at the main gate.

The route is a loop that runs in one direction.

1. **Danubi Plaza (다누비광장)**: departure
2. **Observatory (전망대)** (1.7 km)
3. **Yeongdo Lighthouse (영도등대)** (200 m)
4. **Taejongsa Temple (태종사)** (600 m)
5. **Danubi Plaza**: arrival (1.3 km)

You can get off and back on at any stop. The bus stops running in bad weather such as rain or a typhoon. Tickets go quickly on weekend afternoons, so head to the ticket office as soon as you arrive. If you are going on a Monday or Tuesday, plan to walk from the start and wear trainers. The observatory and the lighthouse are only 200 m apart, so that stretch is quicker on foot anyway.

## Taejongdae entrance fee and opening hours

There is no entrance fee. Entry became free on September 1, 2006, and from then on ordinary cars were kept out and the Danubi Train started running.

| Period | You can enter from | You have to be out by |
|---|---|---|
| November–February | 05:00 | 24:00 |
| March–October | 04:00 | 24:00 |

You can walk the loop road until midnight, but the shoreline, including the pebble beach (자갈마당) below the lighthouse, is closed off from around 8 p.m. because of military operations. The sun sets early in winter. Sunset in Busan between December 3 and 8 is at 17:11 (Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute, checked November 11, 2026). If you want to see the colour of the sea, be at the main gate before 3 p.m.

## What is there to see? The order to walk it

Taejongdae is a park of 1,632,809 m² at the southern tip of Yeongdo (영도). It was designated Scenic Site No. 17 on November 1, 2005, and recognised as a National Geopark on December 6, 2013. Follow the order of the loop bus stops.

{{< figure src="taejongdae-yeongdo-lighthouse.jpg" alt="The white Yeongdo Lighthouse and its maritime culture building on the cliff, with a stairway leading down below" caption="The Yeongdo Lighthouse maritime culture space. The indoor exhibits are closed every Monday (Busan Infrastructure Corporation information) (May 2014) · Photo: Korea Tourism Organization (PhoKo), Kim Ji-ho · KOGL Type 1" >}}

- **Observatory (전망대)**: on the cliff about halfway round the loop road. You can see Oryukdo (오륙도) close by and, on a clear day, Tsushima Island about 56 km away. The shop in the observatory rest area is temporarily closed.
- **Yeongdo Lighthouse (영도등대)**: first lit in December 1906. In 2004 it was turned into a maritime culture space with a gallery, a library, a video room and a natural history exhibition room. The indoor areas are closed on Mondays, January 1, and the days of Seollal and Chuseok. The doors are open if you go between 10 a.m. and 5 p.m. Enquiries: 051-405-1201.
- **Sinseon Rock and Mangbuseok (신선바위·망부석)**: the rocks below the lighthouse. Access is blocked for now because of the risk of falling rocks, so you will have to settle for looking down from above.
- **Taejongsa Temple (태종사)**: a temple beside the loop road. There is an unstaffed convenience store in front of it.
- **Cruise boats**: they leave from the piers and go around the sea off Taejongdae and near Oryukdo for about 40 minutes. Fares and times differ from pier to pier, so phone before you board (Deungdae Cruise, 등대유람선, 051-405-3434).

The way down to the lighthouse is a long flight of steps. You have to climb back up as far as you went down, so if someone in your group has bad knees, it is better to stop at the observatory and turn back.

## How to get to Taejongdae

There is no subway station, so you need a bus. These are the routes and travel times the corporation gives.

| From | Where to board · bus | Travel time |
|---|---|---|
| Busan Station (부산역) | Exit 7 → opposite the Kyowon Building (교원빌딩): 17, 88, 101 | About 40 min |
| Nampo-dong (남포동) | Nampo Station (남포역) Exit 6 → opposite Lotte Department Store (롯데백화점): 8, 17, 30, 88, 186 | About 30 min |
| Seomyeon (서면) | Opposite Judies Taehwa (쥬디스태화): 88 | About 1 hr |
| Gwangalli (광안리) | 41 or 42 → change to 101 at Munhyeon Intersection (문현교차로) | About 1 hr 16 min |
| Haeundae (해운대) | 1003 → change to 88 at Jungang-dong (중앙동) | About 1 hr 21 min |

Get off at the "Taejongdae (태종대)" stop or at the last stop, "Taejongdae Oncheon (태종대온천)". From Haeundae it takes more than an hour, so it makes sense to put Taejongdae on the same day as Nampo-dong and Jagalchi (자갈치). Subway fares and transfers are covered in [how to use the Busan subway]({{< relref "/posts/busan-metro-guide" >}}).

## Parking at Taejongdae

The car parks are open 24 hours and take card payment only.

| Car park | Spaces | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Car Park 7 (제7주차장) | 399 | Cars |
| Car Park 7-2 (제7주차장-2) | 144 | Cars |
| Jayu Land car park (자유랜드 주차장) | 114 | |
| Main gate car park (정문입구 주차장) | 26 | Buses only |

| Vehicle | First hour | Every 10 minutes after that |
|---|---|---|
| Small (under 15 seats) | ₩1,000 | ₩200 |
| Medium (under 25 seats) | ₩1,500 | ₩300 |
| Large (25 seats or more) | ₩2,000 | ₩400 |

The fees are from the Busan Infrastructure Corporation's FAQ. Compact cars, multi-child families and eco-friendly cars get 50% off. Three hours in a regular car comes to ₩3,400, so parking is cheap. Ordinary cars still cannot drive onto the loop road inside the park during the day. In the daytime only vehicles carrying people with reduced mobility can go in, for ₩2,000 per vehicle, and the night-time opening to cars is also ₩2,000 per vehicle. Ask the Busan Tourism Organization on 051-405-8745 what time you can drive in.

## Tips

- **Go between Wednesday and Sunday.** There is no loop bus on Mondays and Tuesdays, so you would have to walk the whole 4.3 km.
- **Leave your bags at the main gate.** There are lockers to the left of the convenience store on the ground floor of the management office building. A large one is ₩1,000 and a small one ₩500, card only.
- **Strollers and wheelchairs are lent out free.** Ask at the tourist information centre at the main gate from 10:00 to 17:00, and bring ID. They are for visitors with a child under 24 months or anyone who has trouble walking. They are not lent out on days with rain, snow or strong wind.
- **Dogs** can come in if you bring a lead and waste bags. They cannot ride the Danubi Train with you.
- **Sunrise** is best seen from Yeongdo Lighthouse and the observatory. In winter you can enter from 05:00, and sunrise in Busan on December 31 is at 07:32 (Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute, checked November 11, 2026). The wind is strong up on the cliffs, so check the [Busan in December clothing post]({{< relref "/posts/busan-december-what-to-wear" >}}) and dress for it.
- Add the [Songdo Marine Cable Car]({{< relref "/posts/songdo-cable-car-guide" >}}) on the same day and you see the sea off both Yeongdo and Songdo in one go.

## FAQ

### Is there an entrance fee at Taejongdae?

No. It has been free since September 2006. About the only thing you pay for is parking.

### When will the Danubi Train run again?

The Busan Tourism Organization's notice only says it is off "until the safety inspection is finished." Call 051-405-8745 the day before you go and they can tell you what is running that day.

### Do I have to pay for the loop bus?

No. You pick up a free ticket at the ticket office and board with it. It is first come, first served.

### Can I go on a Monday?

The park is open. The loop bus and the indoor exhibits at Yeongdo Lighthouse are both off that day, though, so go expecting to walk the full lap.

### Can I walk the whole loop?

Yes. The loop road is 4.3 km and you can walk it until midnight.
