**Yes, you can. There just aren't many, so don't count on it.** Haeundae Blueline Park (해운대블루라인파크) sells **a small number of on-site tickets at the counter, first come, first served**, separate from the online stock. The official notice says they "can sell out early". If your travel date is fixed, book online first and treat the counter as a backup. One thing works the other way round: **discounted fares, such as the senior and Busan resident rates, are sold only at the counter.** (Checked on the official website, November 12, 2026)

## Booking vs walk-in, in one table

| | Online booking | Walk-in |
|---|---|---|
| Where | Booking screen on the official website | Ticket counters at Mipo Station (미포정거장) and Cheongsapo Station (청사포정거장) |
| When it opens | Every Tuesday at 07:00, for one week (Mon–Sun) four weeks ahead | On the day, first come, first served |
| Quantity | Sold by 30-minute entry slot | Small, can sell out early |
| Price | Per capsule: ₩50,000 (2), ₩55,000 (3), ₩60,000 (4) | Same |
| Discounts (seniors, disabled, veterans, Busan residents, Haeundae residents) | Not available | **Counter only**, ID required |
| Winter entry times | First slot 08:30–09:00, last slot 17:30–18:00 | Same |

The price is per capsule, not per person. Four people filling one capsule pay about ₩15,000 each.

## It's the day and you have no ticket

1. **Open the official booking screen first.** On the [booking page](https://www.bluelinepark.com/eng/booking.do), tap Mipo departure and Cheongsapo departure separately and see whether any slots are left for that date. One station can be full while the other direction still has room.
2. **Nothing there? Get to the counter early in the morning.** The first winter entry slot is 08:30, and since on-site tickets go in order of arrival, the morning beats the afternoon. I'd give up on getting a sunset slot (16:30–17:30) at the counter. It saves you the stress.
3. **Still nothing? Take the Beach Train (해변열차).** It runs the same Mipo to Cheongsapo stretch on the same line, and a single ride from Mipo is ₩10,000. There are no reserved seats and you can ride standing, so it's easier to get on than a capsule.

## Exceptions that are easy to miss

- **Cancel on the day and you get half back.** Full refund up to 3 days before the ride, 20% deducted 2 days before, 30% 1 day before, 50% on the day before your entry time. Once your entry time has passed there's no refund, even if you didn't ride.
- **You can't change the date or time.** You have to cancel and book again, so "grab a slot now and move it later" doesn't work.
- **You board only at the station you bought for.** A Mipo ticket won't get you on at Cheongsapo, so go to the counter at the station you want to ride from.
- **If you qualify for a discount, don't buy online.** Online has the standard fare only. The Busan resident and Haeundae resident discounts also apply to alien registration cards (F-4, F-5, F-6).

So if your trip is more than four weeks away, set an alarm for **07:00 on the Tuesday four weeks before**. If that Tuesday has already gone, work through the booking screen, then the morning counter, then the Beach Train.

## Getting to the ticket counter

Mipo Station is about 800 m on foot (10–15 minutes) from **Exit 7 of Jung-dong Station (중동역)** on subway Line 2. Cheongsapo Station has no subway stop nearby, so you take village bus 2, 7 or 10 and get off at the "Blueline Park" (블루라인파크) stop. If you're going for on-site tickets, Mipo is the easier one to reach because the subway gets you there.
