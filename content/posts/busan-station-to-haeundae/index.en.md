**The subway from Busan Station (부산역) to Haeundae Station (해운대역) takes 46 minutes and costs ₩1,800 with a transit card.** You ride Line 1, change once at Seomyeon Station (서면역), and finish on Line 2. If you would rather stay in one seat, **express bus 1003** stops at both Busan Station and the Haeundae Beach bus stop for ₩2,100. The details below come from Busan Transportation Corporation's route search, fare table and timetables, Busan city's fare pages, and the official Busan tourism site (visitbusan.net). I checked them on November 9, 2026.

The subway is the one option with a predictable arrival time. Bus and taxi times change with traffic, so the subway is the only one I give a number for.

## Subway: Line 1, change at Seomyeon, Line 2

This is what Busan Transportation Corporation's route search returns for Busan Station to Haeundae Station on the fastest-route setting.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Route | Line 1 Busan Station → Seomyeon (transfer) → Line 2 Haeundae Station |
| Travel time | 46 min, including 2 min for the transfer |
| Distance | 20.1 km |
| Transfers | 1, at Seomyeon Station |
| Fare | ₩1,800 by card, ₩1,900 QR ticket (Section 2) |

Step by step:

1. At the **Line 1 Busan Station (부산역)** subway stop, board a train **bound for Nopo (노포)**.
2. Get off at **Seomyeon (서면)** and change to **Line 2 bound for Jangsan (장산)**.
3. Get off at **Haeundae (해운대)**. For Haeundae Beach (해운대해수욕장), take **Exit 5** and walk 15 minutes, according to the Busan Tourism Organization.

The subway stop sits on the west side of the railway station. The operator's own station page says it was built there to carry onward passengers from the trains. Both Busan Station and Haeundae Station have **coin lockers and elevators**.

### Fares

Busan's subway charges by distance in two sections. Up to 10 km from your starting station is Section 1, and anything longer is Section 2. Busan Station to Haeundae is 20.1 km, so it falls in **Section 2**.

| Passenger | Transit card or mobile ticket | QR ticket |
|---|---|---|
| Adult (19 and over) | ₩1,800 | ₩1,900 |
| Youth (13 to 18) | ₩1,200 | ₩1,300 |
| Child (6 to 12) | Free | ₩800 |

There is also a **1-day pass for ₩6,000** (adult), sold in the operator's mobile app. It covers unlimited subway rides on the day you first use it. It does **not** work on buses, the Donghae Line, or the Busan-Gimhae Light Rail. Four Section 2 rides would cost ₩7,200, so the pass pays off from the fourth ride.

### First and last trains

These times are from the operator's timetable. Weekday, Saturday and holiday schedules differ slightly, and the operator notes that times can change, so check again if you are travelling late.

| Leg | First train | Last train |
|---|---|---|
| Busan Station toward Seomyeon (Line 1, Nopo-bound) | 05:26 | 23:49 |
| Seomyeon toward Haeundae (Line 2, Jangsan-bound) | 05:36 | 00:00 |
| Haeundae toward Seomyeon (Line 2) | 05:21 | 23:29 (Hopo-bound) |

One more train leaves Haeundae at 23:46, but it terminates at Gwangan (광안) and will not get you to Seomyeon or Busan Station.

## Express bus 1003: one seat, no transfer

The Busan Tourism Organization lists buses 139, 307 and **1003** for the Haeundae Beach stop (해운대해수욕장), and lists **1003** again among the buses that stop at Busan Station (부산역) on its pages for sights near the station. Since 1003 serves both stops, you can board at Busan Station and get off at Haeundae Beach without changing.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Adult fare | ₩2,100 by card, ₩2,200 cash (seated-bus fare) |
| Late-night adult fare | ₩2,500 by card, ₩2,600 cash (late-night seated bus) |
| Where to get off | Haeundae Beach stop (해운대해수욕장) |

The city's route table classes 1003 as an express (seated) bus running between Cheonggang-ri in Gijang and the university hospital stop. That table is dated February 1, 2023. **I could not confirm current first and last departures, frequency or travel time, so I have left them out.** Before you ride, search "1003" in Naver Map or KakaoMap for live arrival times.

## Taxi

Busan city publishes these fares for standard taxis. They took effect on June 1, 2023 and are the rates currently posted.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Base fare | ₩4,800 for the first 2 km |
| Distance charge | ₩100 per 132 m |
| Time charge | ₩100 per 33 seconds when moving under 15 km/h |

No official source gives an estimated fare for Busan Station to Haeundae, so I am not quoting one. The time charge adds up in heavy traffic, and the total can vary a lot. A map app's fare estimate is the best guide.

## Route summary

- **Arriving by train at Busan Station:** go to the Line 1 subway stop on the west side of the station, ride toward Nopo, change at Seomyeon to Line 2 toward Jangsan, and get off at Haeundae.
- **From Haeundae Station to the sea:** Exit 5, then a 15-minute walk.
- **By bus:** board 1003 at the Busan Station stop and get off at the Haeundae Beach stop.

## General tips

These are general tips for this trip. They are not official guidance.

- **On a schedule? Take the subway.** It is 46 minutes whatever the roads are doing.
- **Heavy luggage.** Both Busan Station and Haeundae Station have elevators and lockers.
- **Leave margin late at night.** The last Line 1 train from Busan Station (23:49) leaves very little time for the last Line 2 train from Seomyeon (00:00). Setting off before 23:00 is safer.
- **Hate transfers? Bus 1003.** The trade-off is an arrival time you cannot count on.
- **A group travelling late** might find a taxi easier. The fare is whatever the meter shows.
- **Show the Korean name.** Having 해운대역 or 해운대해수욕장 on your phone screen helps when asking for directions.

## FAQ

### How long is the subway from Busan Station to Haeundae?

46 minutes according to Busan Transportation Corporation's route search. That includes one transfer at Seomyeon and 2 minutes of transfer time.

### How much does it cost?

₩1,800 for an adult with a transit card, or ₩1,900 with a QR ticket. This is the Section 2 fare for trips over 10 km.

### Where do I change trains?

At Seomyeon Station. You arrive on Line 1 (Nopo-bound) and continue on Line 2 (Jangsan-bound).

### Which exit at Haeundae Station is for the beach?

The Busan Tourism Organization gives Exit 5 and a 15-minute walk to Haeundae Beach.

### Is there a way without transferring?

Express bus 1003 stops at both Busan Station and the Haeundae Beach stop. The fare is ₩2,100 by card. Check frequency and travel time in a map app.

### When is the last train?

From Busan Station toward Seomyeon, the last Nopo-bound Line 1 train is at 23:49. From Seomyeon toward Haeundae, the last Jangsan-bound Line 2 train is at 00:00. Going the other way, the last train from Haeundae that reaches Seomyeon is at 23:29 (Hopo-bound).

### Does the 1-day pass work on buses?

No. The ₩6,000 pass is for the subway only. It is not valid on buses, the Donghae Line or the Busan-Gimhae Light Rail.
