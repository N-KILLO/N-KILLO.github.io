**November in Busan is late autumn, and December is early winter: cold, but not Seoul cold.** Going by the Korea Meteorological Administration (기상청, KMA) climate normals, which are 1991 to 2020 averages, Busan's mean temperature is **11.9°C in November and 5.8°C in December**. That is **4 to 6°C warmer** than Seoul on the same basis (7.5°C and 0.2°C). It rains little (50.4mm in November, 33.8mm in December) and clear days are common, but the sea wind is stronger than in Seoul. The sun also sets early, as early as **17:11 in early December**. So pack **layers and a windproof outer layer**; they will do more for you than one thick coat. The numbers come from the KMA Open MET Data Portal and the Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute (한국천문연구원, KASI), checked on November 11, 2026.

## By ten-day period: early November and late December are different seasons

A monthly average doesn't tell you much. Daytime highs on November 1 and November 30 are more than 5°C apart. If your dates are fixed, find your ten days in the table below and ignore the rest.

| Period | Mean | Daytime high | Morning low | Rainfall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Early November (1–10) | 14.3°C | 19.1°C | 10.7°C | 19.7mm |
| Mid-November (11–20) | 11.7°C | 16.3°C | 8.1°C | 15.4mm |
| Late November (21–30) | 9.8°C | 14.5°C | 6.1°C | 15.3mm |
| Early December (1–10) | 7.2°C | 11.7°C | 3.4°C | 13.4mm |
| Mid-December (11–20) | 5.4°C | 10.0°C | 1.7°C | 10.7mm |
| Late December (21–31) | 4.8°C | 9.5°C | 1.1°C | 9.7mm |

In early November the afternoon reaches 19°C and autumn clothes are enough. By late December mornings sit around 1°C, which is full winter. **The dividing line is late November.** Morning lows drop to the mid single digits from then on, so that is when the coat comes out.

## Busan vs Seoul: how much milder is it?

"I heard Busan is warm. Do I still need a puffer jacket?" comes up a lot, so here are the two cities side by side on the same climate normals.

| | Busan Nov | Seoul Nov | Busan Dec | Seoul Dec |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mean temperature | 11.9°C | 7.5°C | 5.8°C | 0.2°C |
| Mean high | 16.6°C | 11.9°C | 10.4°C | 4.2°C |
| Mean low | 8.3°C | 3.5°C | 2.0°C | -3.4°C |
| Monthly rainfall | 50.4mm | 51.1mm | 33.8mm | 22.6mm |
| Mean wind speed | 3.1m/s | 2.2m/s | 3.3m/s | 2.3m/s |
| Monthly sunshine hours | 195.5 hours | 157.3 hours | 205.6 hours | 162.9 hours |

December in Busan (mean 5.8°C) is a little colder than November in Seoul (7.5°C). You usually won't need the long midwinter puffer coat people wear in Seoul, but **the wind in Busan is about 1.4 times stronger.** Dress lightly because of the temperature alone and you will regret it on the beach. Busan also gets about 40 more hours of sun a month, so a sunny spot in the daytime feels warmer than the numbers suggest.

## What to wear, period by period

These outfits are based on the normal temperatures above.

| Period | Day / morning | Outer layer | Underneath |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early November | 19°C / 11°C | Light jacket, cardigan | Long-sleeved T-shirt, shirt |
| Mid-November | 16°C / 8°C | Trench coat, heavier jacket | Knit sweater, sweatshirt |
| Late November | 15°C / 6°C | Coat, light down jacket | Knit sweater + thin base layer |
| Early December | 12°C / 3°C | Coat, puffer jacket | Knit sweater, scarf |
| Mid to late December | 10°C / 1–2°C | Puffer jacket, heavy coat | Fleece-lined base layer, scarf and gloves |

- **Layering is what matters.** Daytime and morning temperatures are 8 to 9°C apart, and the subway, cafes and department stores are well heated. Pieces that are easy to take off and put back on are the most comfortable.
- **For the outer layer, windproof beats thick.** At Haeundae and Gwangalli beaches, observatories and on cruise boats you face the sea directly and get the full wind. A windbreaker or puffer jacket works better than a knit coat the wind passes through.
- **Go one step warmer for evening plans.** Night views, light festivals and drone shows mean standing still for a long time after sunset. Dress for the next row down in the table.
- **Sneakers are enough.** December rainfall is only 33.8mm, so you don't need waterproof boots. Keep one folding umbrella in your bag.
- **It is dry.** Mean humidity in December is 48.1%. Bring lip balm and hand cream.

Climate normals are 30-year averages, so any given day can be a few degrees off. Two or three days before you pack, search "Busan" on the [KMA Weather Nuri site](https://www.weather.go.kr) and look at the forecast low and wind speed once more.

## Temperatures, sunrise and sunset by date (2026)

Temperatures are the climate normals for each date. Sunrise and sunset are the actual 2026 times.

| Date | Daytime high | Morning low | Sunrise | Sunset |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| November 15 | 16.6°C | 8.3°C | 06:58 | 17:18 |
| November 20 | 14.9°C | 6.4°C | 07:03 | 17:15 |
| November 30 | 13.7°C | 5.5°C | 07:12 | 17:12 |
| December 5 | 11.4°C | 3.1°C | 07:17 | 17:11 |
| December 15 | 9.8°C | 1.5°C | 07:24 | 17:13 |
| December 25 | 9.8°C | 1.4°C | 07:30 | 17:17 |
| December 31 | 9.0°C | 0.1°C | 07:32 | 17:21 |

- **December 3 to 8 has the earliest sunset** (17:11). About 28 minutes later (around 17:39) most of the light has left the sky too.
- So **finish outdoor plans such as the beach or alley walks before 16:30**, watch the sunset from 17:00, and move on to night views after 17:40. That order keeps the route simple.
- The temperature falls quickly once the sun is down. Carry the jacket you took off at midday in your hand, not in your bag.
- If you plan to watch the year-end sunrise, it is at 07:32 on December 31. The normal temperature at that hour is around 0°C, so for that one day bring gloves and hand warmers as well.

## Planning your days around this

Now put the weather to use. Days are short in Busan in November and December, and most of what there is to see happens at night.

- **From November 28** the [Haeundae Light Festival]({{< relref "/posts/haeundae-light-festival-2026" >}}) is on. The normal temperature that day is 13.9°C in the daytime and 5.9°C in the morning. You will be standing on the beach, so add a scarf to your coat.
- **From November 26 to December 25** [Christmas Village Busan]({{< relref "/posts/christmas-village-busan-2026" >}}) is held at the Busan Cinema Center (영화의전당). It is an outdoor plaza, so a puffer jacket is more comfortable in December.
- For the sunset, the 16:30 to 17:30 entry slots on the [Haeundae Sky Capsule]({{< relref "/posts/haeundae-sky-capsule-guide" >}}) overlap with it.

## Worth packing

- **One thin, light down jacket.** It is your outer layer in November and goes under a coat in December. It takes up almost no room in a suitcase.
- **A scarf.** On a windy beach it makes the biggest difference to how cold you feel. Gloves from mid-December.
- **Sunglasses.** Winter in Busan has many clear days, and the sun reflects off the sea.
- **A folding umbrella.** Rainy days are rare, but when rain comes it comes with wind. Bring a sturdy one.
- **Hand warmers.** For night views and the sunrise in late December. Convenience stores sell them.

## FAQ

### Do I need a puffer jacket in Busan in November?

A jacket or trench coat is enough until mid-November (daytime 16 to 19°C). From late November mornings drop to around 6°C, so bring out a coat or a light down jacket.

### How cold is Busan in December?

The mean is 5.8°C, with daytime highs of 10.4°C and morning lows of 2.0°C. That is 5 to 6°C warmer than Seoul in December (mean 0.2°C, morning -3.4°C). The mean wind speed is 3.3m/s, though, stronger than Seoul's 2.3m/s, so it feels colder on the beach.

### Does it rain much in November and December?

Not much. Normal monthly rainfall is 50.4mm in November and 33.8mm in December, a sixth to a tenth of midsummer July (326.8mm).

### What time does the sun set in Busan in December?

In 2026 the earliest sunset is 17:11, from December 3 to 8. It is 17:17 on December 25 and 17:21 on December 31. Sunrise gets later through the month, from 07:13 on December 1 to 07:32 on December 31.

### Should I bring a long puffer coat?

It is handy if you have long outdoor plans at night (night views, the sunrise) from mid-December on. If you are mostly out in the daytime, a coat or a regular puffer jacket with a scarf is enough.

### I'm visiting both Seoul and Busan. How should I pack?

Pack for Seoul and wear one layer less in Busan. The December mean temperatures are 5.6°C apart. Do put in a windproof outer layer for Busan.
