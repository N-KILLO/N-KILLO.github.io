The 13th Haeundae Light Festival (해운대빛축제) runs from **Saturday, November 28, 2026 to Sunday, January 10, 2027** at Haeundae Beach (해운대해수욕장) and Haeundae Square on Gunam-ro (구남로). The **lighting ceremony is on November 28 at 18:00 on the sand at Haeundae Beach**. This year's theme is "Deep Dive Haeundae." Everything below comes from the Haeundae-gu district office (Korean only), checked on November 6, 2026.

## What's on display

The district says the layout follows a dive from the surface down to the deep sea, getting brighter as you go. You start on Gunam-ro and end on the beach.

**Gunam-ro (Haeundae Square)**

- A cruise-ship gate and a 30 m light tunnel
- A wave-shaped photo zone, and LED media art on a giant diver sculpture
- 11 Christmas trees, the tallest 6 m. A snow machine is planned for Christmas.

**Haeundae Beach**

- A 12 m ship sculpture, the festival's centerpiece
- Glowing spheres with sea creatures, a submarine, a sea turtle arch
- A giant pearl clam and a golden treasure chest

**Things to do**

- Interactive lights that change color when you touch them
- A lighthouse at the far end of the beach with a wish-message zone and a 2027 New Year photo spot
- Weekend workshops during the festival: glow-in-the-dark ocean bracelets and fishbowl mood lamps (times not announced yet)

## Getting there

Gunam-ro is the street that runs from Haeundae Station down to the beach, so the subway is the easiest option. The district office points visitors to **Line 2, Haeundae Station (해운대역), Exit 3 or 5**.

| From | How |
|---|---|
| Busan Station (subway) | Line 1 to Seomyeon, transfer to Line 2, get off at Haeundae Station, Exit 3 or 5 |
| Busan Station (bus) | Express bus 1001 or 1003 to Haeundae Station or Haeundae Beach |
| Gimhae Airport | City bus 307 or Limousine Bus 2 |

## General tips

These aren't official. They're the usual things worth knowing for a winter night festival.

- Opening night gets crowded. If you want to see the ceremony, reach the beach before 18:00.
- The wind off the sea is cold, and stronger on the sand than on Gunam-ro. Bring gloves and a scarf.
- Walk from the station toward the sea. The displays are arranged in that order.
- The workshops only run on weekends, and their times haven't been posted. Check the district site first.

## FAQ

### When is the Haeundae Light Festival 2026?

November 28, 2026 (Saturday) to January 10, 2027 (Sunday).

### What time is the lighting ceremony?

18:00 on November 28, on the sand at Haeundae Beach.

### What time do the lights turn on each night?

Last year they were 18:00–23:00. Check with the district office for 2026.

### Is there an entry fee?

Ask the Culture & Tourism Division (+82-51-749-4062) or the 1330 hotline.

### Which subway exit?

Line 2, Haeundae Station, Exit 3 or Exit 5.
