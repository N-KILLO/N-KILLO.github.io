**Same railway line, different height and different price.** The Haeundae Blueline Park Sky Capsule (블루라인파크 스카이캡슐) is a capsule on an elevated rail that you share **only with your own group** (Mipo ↔ Cheongsapo, 2 km, about 30 minutes one way, from ₩50,000 per capsule). The Beach Train (해변열차) runs on the tracks underneath and goes **4.8 km, all the way to Songjeong** (₩10,000 per person for a single ride, no reserved seats). For two people going from Mipo to Cheongsapo, that's ₩50,000 by capsule and ₩20,000 by train. (Checked on the official Blueline Park website, November 13, 2026)

## Sky Capsule vs Beach Train, in one table

| | Sky Capsule | Beach Train |
|---|---|---|
| Route | Mipo ↔ Cheongsapo, 2 km | Mipo ↔ Cheongsapo ↔ Songjeong, 4.8 km |
| Ride time | About 30 minutes one way | About 25 minutes one way, Mipo to Songjeong |
| Price | **Per capsule**: ₩50,000 (2), ₩55,000 (3), ₩60,000 (4) | **Per person**: single ride ₩10,000 (from Mipo or Songjeong), two rides ₩14,000, all-stations pass ₩16,000 |
| Seats | Your group only, up to 4 people | No reserved seats, you may have to stand |
| Getting off midway | No (one way, non-stop) | With the two-ride ticket or all-stations pass you can get off at the observatory stops and board again |
| Booking time | Entry time in 30-minute slots | You pick only the time of your first ride |
| Last run in winter (Nov–Feb) | Entry 17:30–18:00 | 19:00 from Mipo and Songjeong |

The capsule is the slower one. It takes 30 minutes to cover 2 km, which is close to walking pace, so you get that much longer looking at the sea. The train covers more than twice the distance in less time.

## How big is the gap for 2 or 4 people?

These are one-way prices from Mipo to Cheongsapo.

| Group | Sky Capsule | Beach Train, single ride |
|---|---|---|
| 2 people | ₩50,000 (₩25,000 each) | ₩20,000 |
| 3 people | ₩55,000 (₩18,300 each) | ₩30,000 |
| 4 people | ₩60,000 (₩15,000 each) | ₩40,000 |

The per-person price of a capsule drops fast as the group gets bigger. For two it costs 2.5 times the train, but for four the gap is ₩5,000 each, so a family of four will feel the capsule price a lot less.

## So which one should you ride?

- **Sky Capsule**: for couples and families who want to take photos without anyone else in the cabin. The catch is that it ends at Cheongsapo, and tickets are harder to get than for the train.
- **Beach Train**: if you want to go as far as Songjeong, get off and walk at Haewol Skywalk (해월전망대) or Daritdol Skywalk (다릿돌전망대), or ride after dark. It keeps running until 19:00, after the last capsule entry has closed.
- **Both**: the official package (one-way capsule + Beach Train all-stations pass) is ₩73,000 for 2, ₩92,000 for 3 and ₩111,000 for 4. Bought separately it comes to ₩82,000 for two, so you save ₩9,000.

If you have to pick one, here's how it works out. With 2 or 3 people on a budget, take the Beach Train. With 4 people, or if you feel you can't come to Busan and skip that capsule, ride the capsule one way and take the train only for the way back.

## Getting to the boarding point

You can board both at **Mipo Station (미포정거장)**. It's about 800 m on foot (10–15 minutes) from **Exit 7 of Jung-dong Station (중동역)** on subway Line 2. The capsule boards only at the station you booked, so check whether your ticket is for Mipo or for Cheongsapo Station (청사포정거장).
