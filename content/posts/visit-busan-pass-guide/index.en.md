**The Visit Busan Pass (비짓부산패스) is a Busan sightseeing pass that only non-Korean visitors can buy and use.** There are four kinds: the 24-hour pass at ₩55,000, the 48-hour pass at ₩85,000, Big 3 (you pick three attractions) at ₩45,000, and Big 5 (you pick five) at ₩65,000. Korean nationals can neither buy it nor use it, so if you are travelling with Korean friends or family, they will need their own tickets. Everything below is from the official Visit Busan Pass site, checked on November 14, 2026.

## At a glance

| Pass | Price | Free admission | Valid for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **24-hour pass** | ₩55,000 | All of Groups A and B, once per attraction | 24 hours from first entry |
| **48-hour pass** | ₩85,000 | All of Groups A and B, once per attraction | 48 hours from first entry |
| **Big 3** | ₩45,000 | 1 from Group A + 2 from Group B | 180 days from first entry |
| **Big 5** | ₩65,000 | 2 from Group A + 3 from Group B | 180 days from first entry |

The card and mobile versions cost the same. Every pass has to be used within one year of purchase, and a Big 3 or Big 5 expires once that year is up even if some of the 180 days are left.

If you plan to pack everything into a day or two, get a time pass. If your schedule is loose, get Big 3 or Big 5. The Big passes last 180 days, so on a four-day trip you can use one attraction a day.

## It's for non-Korean visitors only

Every product on the official sales page carries this notice in Korean: the Visit Busan Pass is a product for foreigners only, Koreans cannot buy or use it, and they may be refused entry if they present it at a partner attraction.

You also have to show your passport or ID together with the voucher when you pick up a card pass. Buy passes only for the non-Korean people in your group, and have any Korean companions buy a separate ticket at each attraction.

## 24-hour and 48-hour passes: everything, inside the time limit

You get into every Group A and Group B attraction once for free. The clock starts **the moment you get your ticket at the first free attraction**. The official example: enter your first attraction at 9:00 a.m. on January 1 and the 24-hour pass runs until 8:59 a.m. on January 2, the 48-hour pass until 8:59 a.m. on January 3.

So starting in the evening costs you. Start at your first attraction in the morning and you get two stretches of daytime (three with the 48-hour pass).

## Big 3 and Big 5: pick a few, take your time

- **Big 3**: 1 from Group A, 2 from Group B
- **Big 5**: 2 from Group A, 3 from Group B

Divided per attraction, Big 3 works out to ₩15,000 each and Big 5 to ₩13,000. Add up the regular prices of the places you want to go, and buy the pass if the total is higher. The Haeundae Beach Train in Group B, for example, costs ₩10,000 for a regular single ride, so if you only pick places like that you gain nothing. The maths works when you put a high-priced attraction in the Group A slot.

## Card vs mobile

| | Card | Mobile |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Same | Same |
| How you get it | Pick up a physical card at an exchange point | Use it in the app right away |
| Entry | Show the card at the ticket office | Show the QR code on the "My Pass" screen |
| Transit card | Top it up and ride the subway and buses | No |

With the mobile pass you cannot get in with a screenshot of the QR code, a printout or a PDF. You have to show the app screen itself. It cannot be swapped for a physical card either.

The card pass has an EZL (이즐) transit card built in. Top it up at a convenience store or subway station and you can tap it on subways and buses across Korea, and as long as the card is intact it keeps working as a transit card after the pass period ends. Transport fares are not included in the pass price. **You have to top it up yourself.**

If you do not have a transit card yet, the card pass is the better choice, because you get the transfer discount from the moment you leave Gimhae Airport for the city. The reason is in my post on [getting from Gimhae Airport to Busan Station, Seomyeon and Haeundae]({{< relref "/posts/gimhae-airport-to-busan" >}}).

## Where to buy and pick up

**Online**: sign up and buy on the official site (visitbusanpass.com) or in the VISIT BUSAN PASS app. You pay with a **credit or debit card issued outside Korea**. If you bought a card pass online, take the voucher from your email or My Page and your passport (ID) to an exchange point and collect the physical card. You cannot enter attractions until you have it.

**Sales and exchange points** (mostly where you arrive in Busan)

| Place | Location | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Visit Busan Pass counter, Busan Station (official) | KTX Busan Station (부산역), 2nd floor | Daily 09:00–18:00 |
| Busan Station kiosk | Busan Station 2nd floor, near Exit 7 | Open all year |
| Busan Tourist Information Center, Gimhae Airport | Gimhae International Airport (김해국제공항) | Daily 10:00–17:00 (lunch 12:00–13:00) |
| WiFi Dosirak, Gimhae Airport | 1st floor, Exit 3 | Daily 06:00–10:00, 17:00–21:30 |
| Gimhae Airport kiosk | 1st floor, next to Exit 4 | Open all year |
| Olive Young Nampo Town | 40 BIFF Gwangjang-ro, Jung-gu (중구 비프광장로 40) | Daily 10:00–23:00 |
| Olive Young Gwangalli Beach | 235 Gwanganhaebyeon-ro, Suyeong-gu (수영구 광안해변로 235) | Daily 11:30–22:30 |

The Gimhae Airport tourist information center closes on the day of Seollal and the day of Chuseok. The pass is also sold at hotels and souvenir shops in Haeundae, Seomyeon, Songdo and Yeongdo, and online purchases can be collected at WiFi Dosirak in Incheon Airport Terminals 1 and 2 and at Gimpo Airport. The full list is in the [official purchase guide](https://www.visitbusanpass.com/visitBusanPass/purchase).

If you land at Gimhae on a daytime flight, go to the tourist information center. If you arrive early in the morning or in the evening, go to WiFi Dosirak at Exit 3 or the kiosk next to Exit 4. If you come by KTX, the counter on the 2nd floor of Busan Station is the simplest.

## Refunds

- Only passes that have never been used can be cancelled, **within 180 days** of payment.
- A pass counts as used if you have entered an attraction, topped up the card, or tapped it as a transit card.
- If you have already collected a card pass, you have to return it **with the box unopened**, along with the receipt, to the place where you got it.
- Once a card pass is registered in the app, it cannot be refunded.
- Lost passes are not refunded.
- Passes bought through a travel agency or booking site follow that seller's rules.

If your plans are not settled, carry the card around with the box unopened and open it in front of your first attraction.

## Getting there: where to pick up the pass

- **Busan Station**: get off the KTX and go up to the 2nd-floor concourse, where the official counter is. By subway it is Busan Station on Line 1.
- **Gimhae International Airport**: international terminal, 1st-floor arrivals. To get into the city, take the light rail and change to Line 2 at Sasang Station (사상역).

## When it's worth buying

- **You are packing two days with sightseeing** → the 48-hour pass. Start at your first attraction in the morning.
- **You have three or four paid attractions you already want to visit** → Big 3 or Big 5. Put the expensive one in the Group A slot.
- **You are mostly going to places with no admission fee, like the beaches, the markets and Gamcheon Culture Village (감천문화마을)** → skip the pass and just buy a transit card.
- **There is a Korean in your group** → the pass does not cover them, so work out their tickets attraction by attraction.

## FAQ

### Can Koreans buy the Visit Busan Pass?

No. It is for foreigners only, and the official site says a Korean who presents it may be refused entry at partner attractions.

### Does the Visit Busan Pass give me free subway rides?

No. The card pass only has a transit card function, and you have to load money onto it before you can ride. The mobile pass cannot be used as a transit card.

### Can I ride the Sky Capsule with the Visit Busan Pass?

What is on the list is the Haeundae Beach Train. Book the Sky Capsule separately with Blueline Park.

### How long do I have to use a Big 3?

180 days from the day you enter your first attraction. It expires one year after purchase, though, no matter how many days are left.

### Who do I contact with questions?

The Visit Busan Pass customer center at +82 1660 1122 (09:00–18:00, all year) or by email at visitbusanpass@tourpass.kr.
