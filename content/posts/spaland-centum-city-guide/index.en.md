**Spa Land Centum City (스파랜드 센텀시티) is open every day 09:00–22:00, with last entry at 21:00. Admission is ₩26,000 for adults and ₩21,000 for students, and the price is the same on weekdays, weekends and public holidays.** One admission covers up to 4 hours, and every hour past that costs ₩5,000 more. Centum City Station (센텀시티역) on subway Line 2 connects straight into the Shinsegae department store, so you can get there on a rainy day without an umbrella. The price, hours, parking and transport details below are from the official Shinsegae Centum City (신세계백화점 센텀시티점) pages, checked on November 20, 2026.

Spa Land is a hot spring bathhouse and jjimjilbang (Korean sauna) inside the department store. The 1st floor has 18 hot spring baths, fed by two kinds of natural hot spring water pumped up from 1,000 m underground, and 13 themed sauna rooms. The 2nd floor has rest areas and restaurants. The department store opens at 10:30, but Spa Land takes guests from 09:00.

## Spa Land prices

| Category | Price | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Adults | ₩26,000 | Born in 2007 or earlier |
| Students | ₩21,000 | Born 2008–2019 |

- The price is the same on weekdays, weekends and public holidays.
- Entry is **from elementary school age**, and elementary schoolers have to go with a guardian of the same sex.
- The student price goes by **birth year**, whatever your actual age on the day.

Preschool children are not allowed in. If you are traveling with a small child, leave it off the plan and save it for a day when the adults are out on their own.

### How much extra past 4 hours?

| Situation | Time allowed | Extra charge |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Up to 4 hours | None |
| Over 4 hours | Each extra hour | ₩5,000 |
| You spend ₩10,000 or more inside (admission not counted) | Up to 6 hours | None |

Four hours goes quickly once you soak in the baths, try three or four sauna rooms and take a nap. If you plan to stay long, you are better off spending over ₩10,000 at a restaurant or cafe on the 2nd floor. Two extra hours would cost ₩10,000 anyway, so the same money buys you a meal and the full 6 hours.

If you bring a weekday voucher on a weekend or public holiday, you pay ₩6,000 extra. If someone gave you a voucher, it is simplest to use it on a weekday.

## Hours and last entry

- Open: **09:00–22:00**
- Last entry: **21:00**

Walk in at 21:00 and you get one hour. To use the full 4 hours you need to be in before 18:00. If you were going to eat dinner in Haeundae and then head over, flip the order: Spa Land first, dinner after. You get more for your money that way.

The department store has closed days (in November 2026 it was the 9th). If your visit might land on one, call 1668-2850 beforehand and ask whether Spa Land is open.

## Can I bring a suitcase?

Big suitcases cannot go into the locker room. You leave them in the **luggage lockers** in front of the ticket desk before you go in.

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| First 5 hours | ₩3,000 |
| Each extra hour | ₩1,000 |

The lockers inside only take personal things like a bag and your clothes. The luggage lockers start at 5 hours, which leaves room to spare on a 4-hour admission. That is why Spa Land works well for the spare half day between checking out and catching a flight or train.

## What's inside

{{< figure src="shinsegae-centum-city-atrium.jpg" alt="The central hall inside Shinsegae Centum City, open through several floors" caption="Inside Shinsegae Centum City. Spa Land is on the 1st floor of this building and is open 09:00–22:00 (official guide, November 2026) (August 2012) · Photo: Korea Tourism Organization (PhoKo), Kim Ji-ho · KOGL Type 1" >}}

| Floor | Facilities |
|---|---|
| 1st floor, sauna | 18 hot spring baths, Finnish sauna, Roman sauna, steam sauna, Bade pool |
| 1st floor, jjimjil zone | Hwangto (yellow clay) Room, Charcoal Room, Salt Room, Bulgama (kiln sauna), Ice Room, Wave Dream Room, Body Sound Room, SEV Room, Roman Room, Hammam Room, Pyramid Room, Bali Room |
| 2nd floor | Relax Room, aesthetic salon, restaurant, cafe and drink bar |

- The **hot spring water** comes in two kinds, one with calcium chloride and one with sodium chloride. Each bath is a different temperature.
- The **Bade pool** has four jets: a body massage you take standing, a dream bath you lie in, a bench jet you sit on, and a bubble jet for the soles of your feet.
- The **Bulgama** is the hottest room. The official guide says to go in for short rounds, 10 minutes two or three times or 5 minutes three to five times, instead of sitting it out.
- The **Ice Room** has a heated floor, so your feet do not get cold. It is where you go after a hot room.
- The **Relax Room** is for lying down with the TV or a magazine.

If this is your first jjimjilbang, warm up in the hot spring baths, then start with a mid-temperature room like the Salt Room or the Hwangto Room. Keep the Bulgama short and for the end, cool off in the Ice Room, and you will be fine.

## How to get there

The **subway** is the easiest way. Get off at **Centum City Station** on Line 2 and the station leads straight into the Shinsegae department store. It is three stops from Haeundae Station, and from Seomyeon Station you take Line 2 toward Jangsan without changing trains. From Busan Station, take Line 1 and change once to Line 2 at Seomyeon. Fares and transfers are in my guide to [using the Busan subway]({{< relref "/posts/busan-metro-guide" >}}).

**Buses** stop at Shinsegae Department Store (신세계백화점 앞).

| Type | Buses |
|---|---|
| Regular | 5-1, 39, 40, 63, 141, 155 |
| Seated | 307 |
| Express | 1001, 1002 |

Bus 307 comes from Gimhae Airport. If you fly out in the evening, you can rest at Spa Land and take the same bus to the airport ([how to get from Gimhae Airport to downtown Busan]({{< relref "/posts/gimhae-airport-to-busan" >}})).

## Parking

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Spa Land guests | 4 hours free |
| You spend ₩10,000 or more inside (admission not counted) | 6 hours free |
| Department store parking, standard rate | First 30 minutes free, then ₩1,000 per 10 minutes |
| Paying in advance | Extra charge if you leave more than 30 minutes after paying |

The free parking runs exactly as long as the admission: 4 hours. Count the time to park, go up and buy a ticket, then come out and get dressed, and the moment you use your full 4 hours inside, the parking meter starts. Past that it is ₩6,000 an hour, so if you drove, leave at around three and a half hours or eat inside and stretch it to 6. On weekends the line just to get into the Centum City parking lot is long, and with the station attached to the building, the subway is the less stressful choice.

## FAQ

### Do I need a reservation?

The official page lists no reservation process. You buy a ticket at the ticket desk and go in. If you are coming as a group of 10 or more, call 1668-2850 first.

### Can children go in?

From elementary school age, yes. Elementary schoolers have to be with a guardian of the same sex, so a father with a daughter or a mother with a son, just the two of them, will have trouble.

### Can I stay overnight?

No. It closes at 22:00, so it cannot stand in for a night's accommodation the way a 24-hour jjimjilbang can.

### Is it good in winter?

It is indoors, so the weather does not matter, and you get in from the station without stepping outside. It suits the evening of a day spent out in the sea wind. For winter temperatures, see [Busan weather in November and December]({{< relref "/posts/busan-november-december-weather" >}}).

### What else is nearby?

[Busan Museum of Art]({{< relref "/posts/busan-museum-of-art-guide" >}}) (부산시립미술관) is at BEXCO Station (벡스코역), one subway stop away. Do the museum in the morning and Spa Land in the afternoon and the whole day is indoors.
