**It's bus 181.** Take city bus 181 from Haeundae Station (해운대역) on subway Line 2, get off at **"Yonggungsa Entrance (용궁사 입구)"** and walk from there to Haedong Yonggungsa Temple (해동용궁사). The bus comes every 20 minutes. If you hate transfers, this is the simplest way to go. (Official Haedong Yonggungsa website and Visit Busan, the Busan Tourism Organization's site, checked November 26, 2026.)

## Bus numbers by starting point, in one table

| Starting from | Bus to take | Stop to get off at |
|---|---|---|
| Haeundae Station, Line 2 | Bus 181 (every 20 minutes) | Yonggungsa Entrance |
| Entrance to Songjeong Beach (송정해수욕장) | Bus 181 · village bus 9 (Gijang-gun) | Yonggungsa Entrance |
| Osiria Station (오시리아역), Donghae Line, Exit 1 | Bus 139 · express bus 1001 | Yonggungsa·National Institute of Fisheries Science (용궁사·국립수산과학원), then a 15-minute walk |

Coming from Seomyeon or Busan Station, take Line 1 and change to Line 2 at Seomyeon Station (서면역). On Line 3, change to Line 2 at Suyeong Station (수영역). Either way, ride to Haeundae Station and catch bus 181 there.

Twenty minutes is a long wait when one has just pulled away in front of you. Before you leave your hotel, search "181번" in Naver Map and see where the bus is.

## When Osiria Station beats bus 181

If you're staying around Centum City or BEXCO instead of Haeundae, the Donghae Line is easier. Come out of Exit 1 at Osiria Station, take bus 139 or express bus 1001 from the "Osiria Station (오시리아역)" stop, get off at "Yonggungsa·National Institute of Fisheries Science" and walk 15 minutes. You can transfer to the Donghae Line at BEXCO Station (벡스코역) on Line 2.

Whichever way you come, there's a fair walk after the bus, and inside the temple you go down the 108 steps and have to climb back up. With luggage or older family members, calling a taxi from Haeundae or Songjeong will save you the trouble.

## What time should I leave in winter?

The temple opens at 04:30 every day, and entry is until 18:50 (per Visit Busan). But in early December the sun sets in Busan at 17:11. You go there to see the colour of the sea, so I'd **get on the bus in Haeundae before 2 pm**. There is no admission fee.
