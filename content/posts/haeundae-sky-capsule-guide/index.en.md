**Book the Sky Capsule in advance.** Tickets go on sale on the official website **every Tuesday at 07:00 (KST) for the week starting four weeks later (Monday to Sunday)**, and only a small number of same-day tickets are sold at the counter. The price is **per capsule, not per person: ₩50,000 for 2, ₩55,000 for 3, ₩60,000 for 4** (one way, Mipo ↔ Cheongsapo, about 30 minutes). From November to February the **last entry slot is 17:30–18:00**, and Busan's sunset in late November and December is between **17:11 and 17:21**, so the 16:30 and 17:00 slots overlap with it. Everything below comes from the Haeundae Blueline Park official site (bluelinepark.com), the Busan Tourism Organization (visitbusan.net) and the Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute's monthly sun tables. I checked it all on November 10, 2026.

## Sky Capsule vs Beach Train: what's the difference?

Both run on the old Donghae Nambu railway line and both are operated by Haeundae Blueline Park (해운대블루라인파크). The Busan Tourism Organization describes the **Beach Train** as a tourist train that "offers transportation" along the 4.8 km coast between Mipo, Cheongsapo and Songjeong, and the **Sky Capsule** as a small automatic capsule that runs the 2 km between Mipo and Cheongsapo on a rail 7 to 10 metres above the ground.

- The **Sky Capsule** is private: only your group rides in it, up to 4 people. It runs one way only, and you board at the station you booked (Mipo or Cheongsapo).
- The **Beach Train** is shared. There are no assigned seats, so you may stand. With a two-ride or all-stops ticket you can get off at the minor stops (Dalmaji Tunnel, Haewol Skywalk, Daritdol Skywalk, Gudeokpo) and get back on.
- The official **package** is one Sky Capsule ride plus an all-stops Beach Train pass. It is built around riding the capsule one way and the train back.

- Discounts are **only available at the ticket counter** with ID. The resident discounts also apply to foreign residents holding an F-4, F-5 or F-6 visa with a Busan or Haeundae address.

## Hours and last entry (winter, November to February)

### Sky Capsule entry slots

The winter capsule timetable has **19 slots of 30 minutes each**. Your booked time is an **entry window**, not a departure time: you enter during those 30 minutes and board in order.

| Slot | Entry time |
|---|---|
| 1 | 08:30–09:00 |
| 2 to 18 | Every 30 minutes from 09:00 |
| 19 (last) | 17:30–18:00 |

Nobody is admitted after the last entry window. Boarding can run late on weekends, holidays or during inspections, and if you arrive more than 30 minutes after your booked time you may be refused without a refund.

### Beach Train first and last departures

| From | First | Last |
|---|---|---|
| Mipo, toward Songjeong | 09:00 | 19:00 |
| Cheongsapo, toward Songjeong | 09:09 | 19:09 |
| Cheongsapo, toward Mipo | 09:12 | 19:12 |
| Songjeong, toward Mipo | 09:00 | 19:00 |

- Ticket sales close **10 minutes before the last train**, and boarding closes 10 minutes before each departure.
- The base frequency is 3 trains an hour. The winter Mipo timetable shows 30 departures a day at 15 to 30 minute intervals.
- Trains run in the rain. They may stop for heavy rain or typhoons, and hours can change without notice for safety checks.

## Booking vs buying on site

**Short version: book the Sky Capsule online. Booking the Beach Train is safer, but on-site tickets exist.** This is what the official booking page says.

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| Release | **Every Tuesday at 07:00 KST**, for the week (Mon–Sun) four weeks ahead |
| Where | Official site only: [bluelinepark.com/booking.do](https://www.bluelinepark.com/booking.do) (Korean, English and Chinese pages) |
| Ticket | Mobile ticket by KakaoTalk or email. The QR code is your boarding pass, the barcode is your parking discount |
| On-site tickets | A **small quantity** sold first come, first served at the counter, separate from online stock. Can sell out early |
| Discounts | Counter only, with ID |
| Changes | Not possible. Cancel and rebook |

### Cancellation and refunds

| When you cancel | Refund |
|---|---|
| 3 days before | Full refund |
| 2 days before | 80% |
| 1 day before | 70% |
| On the day, before your slot | 50% |
| After your slot has passed | No refund, even if you did not ride |

The site describes the Sky Capsule as a product "used at a designated boarding time", which is why booking is the default. Third-party travel platforms also sell tickets, but this article only covers the official channel.

## Departing from Mipo vs Cheongsapo

| | Mipo Station (미포정거장) | Cheongsapo Station (청사포정거장) |
|---|---|---|
| Address | 13 Dalmaji-gil 62beon-gil, Haeundae-gu | 116 Cheongsapo-ro, Haeundae-gu |
| Nearest subway | Line 2 **Jung-dong Station (중동역) Exit 7**, about 800 m, 10–15 min walk | None. Village bus 2, 7 or 10 to the "Blueline Park" stop |
| From Haeundae Beach | Right at the east end of the beach | Reach it by train or capsule |
| Parking | Blueline Park Mipo car park (tower type) | **No dedicated parking** |
| Capsule direction | Mipo → Cheongsapo | Cheongsapo → Mipo |

**Which side is the sea on?** On the official route map the track follows the coast eastward and the sea is to the south. So **from Mipo to Cheongsapo the sea is on your right; from Cheongsapo to Mipo it is on your left.** The sun sets in the west, toward Haeundae Beach and the Marine City towers, so **the Cheongsapo → Mipo direction faces the sunset**. This is a general tip read off the map, not an official recommendation. Seating and the actual view depend on the weather and the capsule.

## A round trip: capsule one way, train back

The official package already has this shape (capsule one way + all-stops train pass). On the booking screen you choose a **departure point (Mipo, Cheongsapo or Songjeong)** and **which ride comes first**.

1. Board the Sky Capsule at **Mipo Station**. About 30 minutes later you arrive at **Cheongsapo**.
2. From Cheongsapo, take the Beach Train with the all-stops pass to **Daritdol Skywalk, Gudeokpo and Songjeong**, then ride back toward Mipo. The pass lets you get off once at each stop.
3. The reverse also works: ride the capsule from Cheongsapo back to Mipo (choose Cheongsapo as the capsule boarding point in the package).

If you start at Songjeong there is no capsule there, so the official instructions tell you to pick the Beach Train as your first ride. The site also warns that a long wait for the capsule can make you miss the last train, so leave a buffer if you go near sunset.

## Sunset times in November and December

These are sunset times for Busan from the Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute's monthly table, based on the official almanac. Compare them with the last winter capsule slot (17:30–18:00).

| Date | Sunset in Busan |
|---|---|
| November 24 | 17:13 |
| November 30 | 17:12 |
| December 3 to 8 | 17:11 (earliest of the year) |
| December 15 | 17:13 |
| December 24 | 17:17 |
| December 31 | 17:21 |

- To see the sunset from a capsule, book the **16:30–17:00 or 17:00–17:30 entry slot**. The ride takes about 30 minutes, so if you board soon after entering you will be over the water while the sun goes down.
- The **Beach Train keeps running until 19:00**, about two hours after sunset, so an after-dark ride is possible.
- Sunset is measured at the horizon. Hills on the Dalmaji side and the weather change what you actually see.

Source: [KASI monthly sunrise/sunset table](https://astro.kasi.re.kr/life/pageView/6) (Korean)

## General tips

Only things that come from the official pages.

- **Remember Tuesday 07:00 Korean time.** A whole week opens at once, four weeks ahead. If you want a specific date, count back four weeks to the Tuesday before it.
- **The Sky Capsule is a timed ticket.** More than 30 minutes late and you may be refused with no refund. Allow about 20 minutes from Haeundae Station to Mipo by bus and on foot.
- **For the Beach Train only your first boarding time is fixed.** Re-boarding is in order of arrival, and Cheongsapo and the minor stops are first come, first served, so you may queue when it is busy.
- **Use the lockers.** Mipo, Cheongsapo and Songjeong have lockers (₩2,000 small, ₩3,000 medium, ₩5,000 large). Cabin-size suitcases are allowed on board, bulky luggage may be refused.
- **Fold strollers, carry pets in a carrier.** Manual wheelchairs are fine on the Beach Train and must be folded for the Sky Capsule. Electric wheelchairs are not allowed on either.
- **Baby changing tables** are in the second-floor women's restrooms at Mipo and Cheongsapo and in the women's restroom by the Songjeong ticket office.
- **It runs in the rain**, but may stop for heavy rain or typhoons. Check the notice board before you go.

## FAQ

### Can I buy a Sky Capsule ticket on the day without booking?

A small number of same-day tickets are sold at the counter, first come, first served, and the park warns they can sell out early. To be sure of a ride, book at the Tuesday 07:00 release.

### Is the Sky Capsule price per person?

No. It is **per capsule**: ₩50,000 for a 2-seater, ₩55,000 for a 3-seater, ₩60,000 for a 4-seater. The inside of every capsule is the same size.

### How much is a Beach Train round trip?

A single ride is one way, so for a round trip you need the two-ride ticket (₩14,000) or the all-stops pass (₩16,000). For children it is ₩11,000 and ₩13,000.

### What is the last ride in winter?

The last Sky Capsule entry window is 17:30–18:00. The last Beach Train leaves Mipo and Songjeong at 19:00, and Cheongsapo at 19:09 (toward Songjeong) and 19:12 (toward Mipo). Ticket sales close 10 minutes before the last train.

### Should I start at Mipo or Cheongsapo?

Mipo is easier by subway (Jung-dong Station Exit 7, 10–15 minutes on foot). For the sunset, the westbound Cheongsapo → Mipo direction faces it, judging from the route map.

### What time is sunset in Busan in late November and December?

According to the Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute: 17:13 on November 24, 17:11 from December 3 to 8, 17:17 on December 24 and 17:21 on December 31.

### Is it included in the Visit Busan Pass?

Check the attraction list on the [Visit Busan Pass official site](https://www.visitbusanpass.com/).

### Can 5 people share one capsule?

The limit is 4. A group of 5 is allowed in a 4-seater only if one of them is 7 or under (born 2020 or later). Otherwise book a 2-seater and a 3-seater.
