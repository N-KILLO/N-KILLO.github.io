**It takes 46 minutes and costs ₩1,800 with a transit card.** At Busan Station (부산역), get on Line 1 bound for Nopo (노포), change once at Seomyeon Station (서면역) to Line 2 bound for Jangsan (장산), and you're at Haeundae Station (해운대역). From Exit 5 it's another 15 minutes on foot to Haeundae Beach (해운대해수욕장), so allow **a little over an hour** from the ticket gates at Busan Station to the sand. (Fares checked November 19, 2026 and travel time checked November 9, both with Busan Transportation Corporation.)

## Time and fare in one table

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Travel time | 46 min (including 2 min for the transfer at Seomyeon Station) |
| Route | Line 1 Busan Station (bound for Nopo) → Seomyeon Station → Line 2 (bound for Jangsan) → Haeundae Station |
| Distance and section | 20.1 km, Section 2 (over 10 km) |
| Adult | ₩1,800 by transit card · ₩1,900 QR ticket |
| Youth (13 to 18) | ₩1,200 by transit card · ₩1,300 QR ticket |
| Child (6 to 12) | Free by transit card · ₩800 QR ticket |
| Haeundae Station → beach | Exit 5, 15 min on foot |

A round trip for two adults comes to ₩7,200 by transit card. If you don't have a card, you can buy a QR ticket from the ticket machine, but you pay ₩100 more every time you ride. The more rides you take, the more the card saves you.

## When the answer changes

- **Four or more rides in a day**: the 1-day pass in the mobile app is ₩6,000. Four Section 2 rides cost ₩7,200, so from the fourth ride the pass is cheaper. It is subway only. You can't use it on buses, the Donghae Line or the light rail.
- **Travelling late at night**: the last Nopo-bound Line 1 train leaves Busan Station at 23:49, and the last Jangsan-bound Line 2 train leaves Seomyeon Station at 00:00. On paper they connect, but it's tight, so leave Busan Station before 23:00. Coming back is earlier still: the last train from Haeundae Station that gets you to Seomyeon is at 23:29 (bound for Hopo, 호포).
- **Taking the first train**: the first Nopo-bound train from Busan Station is at 05:26. Timetables differ a little by day of the week, so for an early morning or last train, check the station timetable once more before you ride.

## How to get there

Come out of the train platforms at Busan Station, go down to the Line 1 Busan Station subway stop and take a train **bound for Nopo**. Get off at **Seomyeon Station**, change to **Line 2 bound for Jangsan**, then leave **Haeundae Station by Exit 5** and walk about 15 minutes to the sea.
