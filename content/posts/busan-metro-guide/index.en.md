**One transit card is all you need for the Busan subway.** There are only two fares, ₩1,600 for trips up to 10 km and ₩1,800 for anything longer, and the discount for changing to a bus applies to cards only. The QR tickets sold at station machines cost ₩100 more per ride and get no transfer discount. The numbers below are from the Busan Transportation Corporation website, checked on November 18, 2026.

## Fares: ₩1,600 for Zone 1, ₩1,800 for Zone 2

The Busan subway has two fare levels, based on the distance from the station where you board to the station where you get off. **Up to 10 km is Zone 1, anything over is Zone 2.**

| Category | Transit card / mobile ticket | QR ticket |
|---|---|---|
| Adult (19 and over), Zone 1 | ₩1,600 | ₩1,700 |
| Adult, Zone 2 | ₩1,800 | ₩1,900 |
| Youth (13 to 18), Zone 1 | ₩1,050 | ₩1,150 |
| Youth, Zone 2 | ₩1,200 | ₩1,300 |
| Child (6 to 12), Zone 1 | Free | ₩700 |
| Child, Zone 2 | Free | ₩800 |

Youth and child fares only apply once a date of birth is registered on the transit card. You can register at a convenience store or on the card issuer's website. Tap an unregistered card and you are charged the adult fare.

If that is hard to picture, remember it this way. Trips inside the city, like Busan Station (부산역) to Seomyeon (서면) or Seomyeon to Gwangalli (광안리), are mostly ₩1,600. Crossing the city, like Busan Station to Haeundae (해운대), is ₩1,800. Wherever you go the difference is ₩200, so there is no point spending time working out fares.

## Three kinds of ticket: transit card, QR ticket, mobile ticket

| Type | Where to buy | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Transit card | Transit card top-up machines inside stations, convenience stores | Transfer discount on buses, the light rail and the Donghae Line. Top up at station ticket machines and top-up machines |
| QR ticket | Station ticket machines | Subway only. ₩100 more |
| Mobile ticket | The 「부산도시철도」 app | Subway only. Foreign visitors can use it as non-members with email verification (adult fare) |

With a QR ticket, hold the printed QR code **face down** on the reader at the gate and walk through. You have to scan it again on the way out, so do not throw it away before you arrive.

Any balance left on a transit card cannot be refunded at stations. You get it back at convenience stores such as 7-Eleven, CU and GS25.

If you are staying two days or more, just buy a transit card at a convenience store first. The same card works on buses, and you pay ₩100 less every time you ride.

## The ₩6,000 1-day pass: worth it from four rides

The 1-day, 3-day and 1-month passes are sold **only in the mobile app**, not at station counters.

| Type | Adult | Youth | How it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-day pass | ₩6,000 | ₩4,000 | The day of your first tap, any distance, unlimited rides |
| 3-day pass | ₩13,000 | ₩9,000 | 3 days from your first tap, any distance, unlimited rides |
| 1-month pass | ₩45,000 | ₩33,000 | 45 rides, any distance |

There is one thing to watch. These passes are good on **subway Lines 1 to 4 only**. They do not work on buses, the Donghae Line or the Busan-Gimhae Light Rail. Unused 1-day and 3-day passes can be refunded in full.

Four ₩1,600 rides come to ₩6,400, so the 1-day pass only pays off if you take the subway four or more times in a day. If your day is a hotel near Busan Station, out to Haeundae, a stop in Seomyeon and back (three rides), the transit card is cheaper. If you plan to mix in buses, the card wins by even more.

## Bus transfers: within 30 minutes, up to twice

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Covered | Subway, city buses (including seated and express buses), village buses, Busan-Gimhae Light Rail, Donghae Line |
| Eligible | Prepaid and postpaid transit cards |
| Time limit | You must board the next ride within 30 minutes of getting off the previous one |
| Number | Up to 2 transfers (three different modes of transport) |
| Fare | Only the single highest fare among the rides you took |

For example, if you get off the subway (₩1,600) and change to a regular bus, the bus reader shows ₩0. The other way round, a regular bus (₩1,550) first and then the subway, costs you just ₩50 more at the subway. Changing to a seated bus adds ₩500.

The catch is that **riding the same mode again is not a transfer**. If you leave the subway and enter it again within 30 minutes, you pay a new base fare. The same goes for taking the same bus number twice.

For places like Gamcheon Culture Village (감천문화마을), where you ride a village bus up from the subway station, the village bus is effectively free as long as you tap your card. The details are in my [Gamcheon Culture Village guide]({{< relref "/posts/gamcheon-culture-village-guide" >}}).

## First and last trains

Times differ at every station. These are the directions visitors use most.

| Leg | First train | Last train |
|---|---|---|
| Busan Station → toward Seomyeon (Line 1, Nopo-bound) | 05:26 | 23:49 |
| Seomyeon Station → toward Haeundae (Line 2, Jangsan-bound) | 05:36 | 00:00 |
| Haeundae Station → toward Seomyeon (Line 2, Hopo-bound) | 05:21 | 23:29 |
| Sasang Station → toward Seomyeon and Haeundae (Line 2, Jangsan-bound) | 05:22 | 23:45 |

The Busan Station and Haeundae Station times were checked on November 9, 2026, and Sasang Station on November 13, in the Busan Transportation Corporation train timetable. For other stations, pick the station name in the [train timetable](https://www.humetro.busan.kr/homepage/default/stationtime/page/list.do?menu_no=1001010301) and the times come straight up.

If you are having dinner in Haeundae and heading back to Seomyeon or Busan Station, be inside the station before 23:00. Miss the 23:29 train and it is a taxi.

## Went through the gate on the wrong side: tap again within 5 minutes

The most common first-timer mistake is walking in to the platform for the opposite direction. There are two rules for this.

- **Tap again at the same station with the same ticket within 5 minutes** and the base fare is waived once. This only works at the station where you first entered.
- If you tap in and then **come back out at the same station within 10 minutes without boarding a train**, the fare is refunded.

So if you realize you went in the wrong way, don't panic. Walk straight back out and tap in at the gate on the opposite side. It is quicker than riding one stop and coming back, and it costs nothing.

## Lockers: from ₩2,000 for 3 hours

These are the base rates (3 hours) for station lockers. The extra charge per hour differs by line.

| Line | Small | Medium | Large | Extra large |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Line 1 | ₩2,000 | ₩3,000 | ₩4,000 | ₩6,000 |
| Line 2 (weekdays) | ₩2,500 | None | ₩4,500 | ₩6,000 |
| Line 2 (weekends) | ₩2,700 | None | ₩4,950 | ₩6,600 |
| Lines 3 and 4 | ₩2,000 | None | ₩4,000 | ₩6,000 |

An extra-large locker is 37 wide × 87 high × 55 cm deep on Line 1, and 42 × 90 × 55 cm on Line 2. The stations with a lot of lockers are Busan Station (135), Seomyeon, Nampo, Jagalchi, Haeundae (85 small, 40 large, 29 extra large), Centum City and Gwangan.

If you have checked out and have hours to fill before an evening train, leave your suitcase at Busan Station and walk around Nampo-dong, or drop it at Haeundae Station and walk along the beach. For a 28-inch suitcase, get an extra-large locker.

## If you left something on the train

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Lost and found center | Seomyeon Station, level B2, in the Line 1/2 transfer passage (next to the subway police unit) |
| Hours | Weekdays 09:00 to 18:00 (closed Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays) |
| Phone | 051-640-7339, 051-678-6191 |

If you notice right after getting off, the fastest thing is to tell the station office at that station straight away. It helps if you remember what time you got off and which car you were in. Found items can also be searched on [Police Minwon 24 (경찰민원24)](https://minwon24.police.go.kr/).

## Bicycles on weekends and public holidays only

You can bring a bicycle on board only on weekends and public holidays, and it goes in the very last car of the train. On weekdays (Monday to Friday) it is not allowed. A folding bike is fine on any day as long as it is folded.

## FAQ

### How much is the Busan subway?

For an adult with a transit card, ₩1,600 up to 10 km and ₩1,800 beyond 10 km. QR tickets are ₩1,700 and ₩1,900.

### Can I buy the 1-day pass at a station?

No, the station machines do not sell it. You buy it in the 「부산도시철도」 app for ₩6,000 and ride as many times as you like on the day you first pass through a gate. It does not cover buses, the Donghae Line or the light rail.

### Do I pay again when I change from the subway to a bus?

If you tapped a transit card, a regular bus you board within 30 minutes of getting off costs nothing extra. QR tickets and mobile tickets are subway-only, so they get no transfer discount.

### Do children ride free?

Children aged 6 to 12 ride free when they tap a transit card registered as a child card. With a QR ticket they pay ₩700 for Zone 1 and ₩800 for Zone 2.

### Is there somewhere to leave a suitcase?

Most stations have lockers. For the base 3 hours, an extra-large locker is ₩6,000 on Line 1, and on Line 2 it is ₩6,000 on weekdays and ₩6,600 on weekends.
