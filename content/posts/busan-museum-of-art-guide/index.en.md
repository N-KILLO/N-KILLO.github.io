**Busan Museum of Art (부산시립미술관) is open Tuesday through Sunday, 10:00–18:00, and closed on Mondays. Admission is free for now, for a limited time.** Last entry is 17:00 for the main building and 17:30 for the annex, Space Lee Ufan (이우환공간). It is about 100 m from Exit 5 of BEXCO (Museum of Art) Station (벡스코(시립미술관)역) on subway Line 2, so it is easy to reach without a car. The hours, admission, exhibition, transport and parking details below are from the official Busan Museum of Art site, checked on November 19, 2026.

Busan Museum of Art reopened after a renovation that started in 2024 and took more than two years. The reopening exhibitions began on September 17, 2026. Right now there are four exhibitions in the main building and one permanent exhibition in the annex, Space Lee Ufan.

## Hours and closed days

| Venue | Tue–Sun | Last entry | Monday |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main building | 10:00–18:00 | 17:00 | Closed |
| Children's Gallery | 10:00–18:00 | 17:00 | Closed |
| Space Lee Ufan | 10:00–18:00 | 17:30 | Closed |

The museum is closed every Monday and on January 1. If Monday is a public holiday, it opens that day and closes the next day.

The main building stops letting people in an hour before closing. With four exhibitions inside, walking in at 17:00 will not get you properly through even one floor. Be inside by 15:30 at the latest, and if you are short on time, leave Space Lee Ufan for last, since it takes visitors for 30 minutes longer.

## Admission: free for now

The official site says both the main building and Space Lee Ufan are **currently free for a limited time**. This is the main building price list that applies once the free period ends.

No end date for the free period is given. Once it goes back to paid, two adults on combined tickets come to ₩14,000, so if you were planning to go anyway, go while it is free.

## Exhibitions on now

| Exhibition | Dates | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Still We are Borne on These Waves: 1945-1953 (그러나 우리는 아직도 이 바다 높은 물결 위에 있다: 1945-1953) | 2026.9.17–2027.2.14 | Main building, 2nd floor galleries |
| Future Museology (퓨처 뮤지올로지) | 2026.9.17–2027.3.14 | Main building, 3rd floor, Gallery 1 |
| Dear All The Yesterdays (이 모든 어제의 미술관에게) | 2026.9.17–2027.3.14 | Main building, 2nd floor, Project Gallery |
| Safe Base (안전기지) | 2026.9.17–2027.5.30 | Children's Gallery, basement level 1 |
| Space Lee Ufan permanent exhibition | Permanent | Annex Space Lee Ufan and the outdoor sculpture park |

- **Still We are Borne on These Waves: 1945-1953** is the reopening exhibition. It shows Korean society from just after liberation through the years around the Korean War with some 250 artworks and some 450 archival items. It is the biggest of the four.
- **Future Museology** is an international exhibition that looks for new roles for the art museum, put together with a consortium of nine museums in Korea and abroad.
- **Dear All The Yesterdays** is a special exhibition from the museum's own holdings. It looks back at the history of the museum building, which went up in 1998, through drawings, photographs and records plus work by five participating artists. The Project Gallery it is held in is a permanent exhibition space added with the reopening.
- **Safe Base** is the Children's Gallery exhibition, in which two artists, NAMELESS and Bang & Lee, deal with the attachment environment of children today.
- **Space Lee Ufan** is the second museum in the world devoted to Lee Ufan alone, after the one on Naoshima in Japan. There are 4 sculptures on the 1st floor and 15 paintings on the 2nd, and the artist took part in designing the building himself.

The 1945-1953 exhibition is the first to close, on February 14. If you only see one, see that first, then cross over to Space Lee Ufan with whatever time is left.

## Floor guide and facilities

| Floor | Facilities |

| 3rd floor | Gallery 1, rooftop garden |
| 2nd floor | Gallery 2, Gallery 3, Project Gallery, BMA Space |
| 1st floor | Information desk, lockers, culture select shop and cafe, multipurpose hall, first-aid room |
| Basement level 1 | Children's Gallery, activity room, classroom, Art Information Center, Low Garden |
| Annex | Space Lee Ufan, 1st–2nd floors (information desk, cafe and shop, galleries) |

- The museum lends out **1 stroller and 1 wheelchair**. They are for visitors with a child under 24 months and for people with disabilities, and you have to leave your ID.
- The **nursing room** has a diaper changing table, a sink and a sofa. Accessible toilets are inside the restrooms on each floor.
- **No food or drink and no pets.** Wheeled sneakers and inline skates are not allowed either.

There is only one stroller to borrow, so if you are coming with a small child you will worry less with your own. Put big bags in the lockers on the 1st floor before you go up.

## How to get there

The **subway** is the easiest way. Leave by **Exit 5 of BEXCO (Museum of Art) Station** on Line 2 and it is about 100 m. That is two stops from Haeundae Station, and from Seomyeon Station you take Line 2 toward Jangsan and get there without changing trains. Fares and transfers are in my guide to [using the Busan subway]({{< relref "/posts/busan-metro-guide" >}}).

**Buses** stop at two places.

| Stop | Buses |
|---|---|
| BEXCO (벡스코) | 31, 39, 40, 63, 100, 100-1, 115-1, 141, 181, 200, 307 |
| Olympic Intersection (올림픽교차로) | 31, 39, 40, 63, 100, 100-1, 115-1, 141, 181, 200, 1001 |

That works out to ₩2,400 an hour and ₩4,800 for two hours. Four exhibitions plus Space Lee Ufan will take you well past two hours, and with the station 100 m away there is not much reason to bring a car. On days with a big event at BEXCO (벡스코), the roads around it back up before you even reach the parking lot.

## FAQ

### Is Busan Museum of Art open now?

Yes. It reopened after the renovation and has been running exhibitions since September 17, 2026.

### Do I need a reservation?

Individual visitors can just turn up. Docent tours also gather at the gallery entrance without booking. Only group visits go through the "Group visit reservation" (단체관람예약) menu on the official site.

### How long does it take to see everything?

There are four exhibitions in the main building and one in the annex. The 1945-1953 exhibition alone has some 250 artworks and some 450 archival items, so to see both the main building and Space Lee Ufan, set aside half a day.

### Is it OK to go on a rainy day?

The exhibitions are indoors, so the weather does not matter. The subway station is 100 m away, which keeps the walk under an umbrella short. For winter weather, see [Busan weather in November and December]({{< relref "/posts/busan-november-december-weather" >}}).

### What else is nearby?

BEXCO is across the street, and Centum City Station (센텀시티역) is one subway stop away. Haeundae Beach (해운대해수욕장) is two stops.
