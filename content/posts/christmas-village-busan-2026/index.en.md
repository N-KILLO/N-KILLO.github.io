**Christmas Village Busan 2026 (크리스마스 빌리지 부산) runs from Thursday, November 26 to Friday, December 25, 2026** on the outdoor plaza of the Busan Cinema Center (영화의전당), 120 Suyeonggangbyeon-daero, Haeundae-gu. It is not open every day. **It opens on 21 days, mostly Thursday to Sunday**, and this year it has switched to **paid, pre-booked tickets**. Online tickets start at ₩5,900 on weekdays and cost ₩14,900 on December 24 and 25. Everything below comes from the official ticket page on YES24 (the sole seller) and the Busan Cinema Center website, checked on November 7, 2026.

## Opening days: not every day

This is the part people get wrong. The event spans a month, but the village is open on only 21 of those days.

| Week | Open |
|---|---|
| Late November | 26 (Thu), 27 (Fri), 28 (Sat), 29 (Sun) |
| December, week 1 | 3 (Thu), 4 (Fri), 5 (Sat), 6 (Sun) |
| December, week 2 | 10 (Thu), 11 (Fri), 12 (Sat), 13 (Sun) |
| December, week 3 | 17 (Thu), 18 (Fri), 19 (Sat), 20 (Sun) |
| December, week 4 | 21 (Mon), 22 (Tue), 23 (Wed), 24 (Thu), 25 (Fri) |

December 21 to 23 are extra weekday openings with weekday hours and prices (16:00–22:00). Those are the only Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday dates.

Rules worth knowing:

- Once inside, you can stay until 22:00 that day. A weekend day ticket only gets you in until 15:00, and **nobody can enter between 15:00 and 16:00 on weekends.**
- Children under 36 months are free. Staff at the ticket booth check proof of birth date (a family relation certificate or a Korean mobile certificate).
- Families with three or more children, at least one under 19, get 20% off the online price at the booth only, with Korean documents. This does not apply to online bookings.
- One person can book up to 4 tickets per time slot and 10 in total. Sales close at 23:59 the day before your visit.
- Show the booking QR code at the on-site ticket booth to get in.

Last year the village was free. The organizers say 450,000 people came and it got crowded, so this year they are charging and capping entry.

### Cancellation fees

| When you cancel | Fee |
|---|---|
| Within 7 days of booking | None |
| Day 8 after booking until 10 days before the visit | ₩4,000 per ticket (up to 10% of the price) |
| 9 to 7 days before the visit | 10% |
| 6 to 3 days before the visit | 20% |
| 2 days before until the cutoff | 30% |

The visit date rule wins over the booking date rule. Even inside the 7-day window, a fee applies if the visit is less than 10 days away.

According to the organizers, the plaza is laid out around a bell tower in four zones. At set times the bell rings, snow falls, a parade goes by and a choir sings.

| Zone | What's in it |

## Getting there

The Busan Cinema Center is a **5-minute walk from Exit 6 or Exit 12 of Centum City Station (센텀시티역) on Line 2**. This is the center's own guidance.

| From | How |
|---|---|
| Subway | Line 2, Centum City Station, Exit 6 or 12, then walk 5 minutes |
| City bus | Get off at Shinsegae Department Store (신세계백화점), walk 5 minutes. Routes 5-1, 36, 39, 40, 63, 115, 139, 141, 155, 181, 307, express 1001 and 1002 |
| Busan Station | Bus 5-1, 40, 139 or 1001 to Shinsegae Department Store |
| Gimhae Airport | Airport limousine bus to Shinsegae Department Store |

Source: [Busan Cinema Center, directions](https://www.dureraum.org/bcc/contents/contentsView.do?rbsIdx=196) (Korean)

## General tips

These aren't official. They're the usual things to think about for a ticketed outdoor event.

- Pick the date and ticket type first. A weekday ticket won't get you in on a weekend. For a weekend afternoon you want the day ticket, for the lights you want the evening one.
- Avoid the 15:00 changeover on weekends. Entry is closed from 15:00 to 16:00, and day-ticket holders must be inside before 15:00.
- December 24 and 25 cost about two and a half times a weekday. If price matters more than the date, December 21 to 23 are the cheapest.
- Take the subway. The car park has 501 spaces, and Centum City traffic is heavy on weekend evenings.
- The plaza sits beside the Suyeong River, and the wind off the water makes evenings feel colder than the forecast.

## FAQ

### When is Christmas Village Busan 2026?

November 26 (Thursday) to December 25 (Friday), 2026, but only on Thursdays to Sundays plus December 21 to 23. That's 21 days in total.

### Is there an entry fee?

Yes. From this year it's pre-booked and paid. Online: ₩5,900 for weekdays and weekend daytime, ₩7,900 for weekend evenings, ₩14,900 on December 24 and 25. Children under 36 months are free.

### Where do I buy tickets?

Only on YES24 Ticket. It's a mobile ticket, and sales close at 23:59 the day before your visit. The site is in Korean. Tickets are also sold at the gate for ₩2,000 more.

### Until what time can I enter?

Weekdays from 16:00. Weekend day tickets 11:00–15:00, weekend evening tickets from 16:00. Once in, you can stay until 22:00.

### Which subway exit?

Line 2, Centum City Station, Exit 6 or 12. About 5 minutes on foot.

### Can I bring my dog?

Yes, on a leash. Strollers are allowed too.
